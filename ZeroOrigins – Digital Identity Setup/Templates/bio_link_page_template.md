## DESIGN NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Background | Dark or white — avoid busy patterns |
| Photo | Square crop, face clearly visible, professional |
| Font | Max 2 fonts. Use your brand font or Inter/Poppins |
| Colors | Stick to your brand palette (2–3 colors max) |
| Link order | Primary CTA first, social last |
| Mobile test | Check on phone before publishing |
