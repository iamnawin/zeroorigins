STEP 1 — FILL THE CONTENT FORM
----------------------------------------------------------------

Open:  Templates/content_collection_form.md

Fill it out completely. Every section has clear instructions.
This form collects everything needed to build your profile.

Do not skip fields. The more specific you are, the better
your digital identity will look.

Time required: 20–40 minutes

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — REVIEW YOUR PROFILE CARD TEMPLATE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Open:  Templates/profile_card_template.md

This is the core asset — your professional summary card.
Use your answers from the content form to fill it in.

This card is what you will use on:
  - LinkedIn About section
  - Email signatures
  - Pitch decks and proposals
  - Bio link pages

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — CHOOSE YOUR STYLE DIRECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Open:  Resources/copy_examples.md

Read through the example tones and styles.
Decide which one feels closest to you:
  → Authoritative / Expert
  → Warm / Relatable
  → Bold / Direct
  → Clean / Minimal

Note your choice. It will shape your bio and copy.
