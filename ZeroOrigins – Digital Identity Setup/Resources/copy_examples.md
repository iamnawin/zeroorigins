## TONE PROFILES

### Tone A — Authoritative / Expert

**Best for:** Consultants, senior advisors, technical specialists, coaches
**Characteristics:** Confident assertions, specific numbers, no hedging

**LinkedIn Headline:**
- Weak: "Marketing Professional | 10 Years Experience | Helping Businesses Grow"
- Strong: "Brand Strategist | I help D2C brands double repeat purchase rate | ₹50Cr+ in attributed revenue"

**One-Line Bio:**
- Weak: "I am a consultant with experience in various industries"
- Strong: "I help B2B SaaS companies reduce churn by 30% in 90 days through structured customer success systems."

**About Section Opening:**
> Most companies lose their best customers not because of a bad product — but because of a broken onboarding experience. I fix that. Over the last 8 years, I have redesigned onboarding flows for 40+ SaaS companies, turning the first 30 days from a churn window into a retention engine.

---

### Tone B — Warm / Relatable

**Best for:** Coaches, community builders, service providers, creators
**Characteristics:** Conversational, story-driven, empathetic

**LinkedIn Headline:**
- Weak: "Life Coach | Helping People Reach Their Potential"
- Strong: "Career Coach | I help first-generation professionals land roles they actually want | 200+ clients placed"

**One-Line Bio:**
- Weak: "I help people with their careers"
- Strong: "I help first-gen professionals figure out what they actually want — and then get it, without burning out."

**About Section Opening:**
> Seven years ago, I landed a "good job" that looked perfect on paper and made me miserable in practice. I quit after 18 months, spent a year figuring out what I actually wanted, and built a career I love. Now I help people skip the miserable part. If you are overqualified for what you are doing and underqualified for what you want, we should talk.

---

### Tone C — Bold / Direct

**Best for:** Founders, growth operators, performance marketers, high-ticket sellers
**Characteristics:** Short sentences, no fluff, strong verbs, outcomes front and center

**LinkedIn Headline:**
- Weak: "Growth Hacker | Startup Enthusiast | Building Cool Stuff"
- Strong: "Growth operator. $0 to $1M ARR twice. Now helping early-stage founders do the same."

**One-Line Bio:**
- Weak: "I am passionate about growth and startups"
- Strong: "I have taken two startups from zero to $1M ARR. Now I do it for founders who are stuck between $10K and $100K MRR."

**About Section Opening:**
> I do not consult. I operate. I take a seat at the table, dig into the numbers, and fix what is actually broken — usually the acquisition funnel or the retention loop. My last two clients 4x'd revenue in under 12 months. Before that, I was VP Growth at [Company]. I work with 3–4 clients at a time, and I am extremely selective.

---

### Tone D — Clean / Minimal

**Best for:** Designers, developers, product people, introverts who hate self-promotion
**Characteristics:** Simple language, few adjectives, no superlatives, lets the work speak

**LinkedIn Headline:**
- Weak: "Product Designer | Creating Beautiful Experiences"
- Strong: "Product Designer — SaaS, fintech, and marketplaces — 7 years"

**One-Line Bio:**
- Weak: "I design things that look good and work well"
- Strong: "Product designer focused on complex workflows in fintech and B2B tools."

**About Section Opening:**
> I design digital products. Mostly fintech and B2B SaaS. Before going independent, I was a lead designer at [Company] for 4 years. My portfolio has projects I can show and a few I cannot. If you have a complex product that needs clear UX, reach out.

## HEADLINE FORMULAS

**Formula 1 — Title + What + Result**
`[Title] | Helping [WHO] [ACHIEVE WHAT] | [PROOF]`
Example: `Content Strategist | Helping SaaS brands generate inbound pipeline | 3M+ words published`

**Formula 2 — Action + Who + Outcome**
`I help [WHO] [OUTCOME] | [DIFFERENTIATOR]`
Example: `I help freelancers raise their rates | without losing clients`

**Formula 3 — Minimal / Clean**
`[Role] — [Specialty] — [Years or Scale]`
Example: `Brand Designer — Luxury and DTC — 9 years`

**Formula 4 — Bold Claim**
`[Specific claim about results] | [What you do to make it happen]`
Example: `Took 2 companies from $0 to $1M ARR | Now helping founders do the same`

## COMMON MISTAKES AND FIXES

| Weak Copy | Why It Fails | Stronger Version |
|---|---|---|
| "Passionate about marketing" | Everyone says this. Means nothing. | "I have run email campaigns that generated ₹2Cr in revenue." |
| "Helping businesses grow" | Too vague. Which businesses? Grow how? | "Helping D2C brands grow email revenue by 40% in 60 days." |
| "10+ years of experience" | Experience is not an outcome. | "10 years building systems that still run without me." |
| "I work with clients worldwide" | Geography is not a value prop. | Leave it out unless geography matters. |
| "Let's connect!" | Weak CTA, no clear next step | "Book a 30-min call to see if I can help." |
