# Tools List
**ZeroOrigins – Digital Identity Setup**

Curated tools for building and maintaining your digital identity.
Each tool is listed with its purpose, cost, and a clear recommendation.

---

## PROFILE AND BIO LINK

| Tool | Purpose | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Linktree** | Bio link page, quick setup | Free / $9/mo | Best for speed |
| **Carrd** | Single-page site, looks professional | $9/year | Best value for polish |
| **bio.site** | Bio link with clean UI | Free | Good free option |
| **Notion + Super.so** | Custom bio page via Notion | $12/mo | Best for power users |
| **Beacons** | Creator-focused link page | Free / $10/mo | Good for content creators |

**Recommendation:** Start with Linktree (free). Upgrade to Carrd when you want a custom domain and cleaner design.

---

## PROFILE PHOTO

| Tool | Purpose | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remini** | Upscale and enhance photos | Free / $5/mo | Quick fix for low-res photos |
| **Remove.bg** | Remove photo background | Free (limited) | Clean background in seconds |
| **Canva** | Crop, resize, format headshots | Free | Good for quick edits |
| **ProPhotos.ai** | AI-generated professional headshots | $29 one-time | If you need a new headshot fast |
| **Aragon AI** | High-quality AI headshots | $29–49 | Best AI headshot quality |

**Recommendation:** Use an actual photo over AI-generated if possible. Aragon is acceptable if you have no good photos.

## DESIGN AND VISUAL IDENTITY

| Tool | Purpose | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canva** | Graphics, banners, social posts | Free / $13/mo | Default choice for most people |
| **Adobe Express** | Quick professional designs | Free / $10/mo | Slightly more polished than Canva |
| **Figma** | Full design system, components | Free | For designers or detail-oriented users |
| **Coolors.co** | Color palette generator | Free | Build your brand palette in 2 minutes |
| **Google Fonts** | Typography | Free | Find fonts that match your brand |
| **Fontpair.co** | Font pairing suggestions | Free | Find two fonts that work together |

**Recommendation:** Canva + Coolors + Fontpair covers 90% of what you need.

## WRITING AND COPY

| Tool | Purpose | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Claude** | Bio writing, refining copy | Free / $20/mo | Best for nuanced, human-sounding copy |
| **ChatGPT** | Drafting bios, brainstorming | Free / $20/mo | Good for volume; needs editing |
| **Hemingway Editor** | Readability check | Free | Flags overly complex sentences |
| **Grammarly** | Grammar and tone check | Free / $12/mo | Useful for final polish |
| **Copy.ai** | LinkedIn headline and bio generator | Free | Quick starting point |

**Recommendation:** Use Claude or ChatGPT to draft, Hemingway to simplify, Grammarly to polish.

## EMAIL AND OUTREACH

| Tool | Purpose | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resend** | Transactional email | Free tier | Developer-friendly |
| **Mailchimp** | Email newsletters | Free up to 500 | Simple newsletter setup |
| **ConvertKit / Kit** | Creator email list | Free up to 1000 | Best for content creators |
| **Wave** | Email signature builder | Free | Clean, professional email signature |
| **HubSpot signature gen** | Email signature generator | Free | Quick option |
