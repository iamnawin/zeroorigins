# Onboarding Checklist
**ZeroOrigins – Digital Identity Setup**

Work through this list top to bottom.
Check each item only when it is genuinely complete — not just started.

---

## STAGE 1 — CONTENT COLLECTION

- [ ] Opened and read README.txt
- [ ] Opened and read START-HERE.txt
- [ ] Filled out every section of `Templates/content_collection_form.md`
- [ ] Reviewed `Guide/example_profile.md` for reference
- [ ] Noted my tone preference from `Resources/copy_examples.md`

---

## STAGE 2 — PROFILE CARD

- [ ] Written my one-line bio (test: sounds human, not corporate)
- [ ] Written my short bio (3–4 sentences)
- [ ] Written my long bio (6–8 sentences)
- [ ] Listed my top 5 skills
- [ ] Have at least 1 testimonial or specific result to include
- [ ] Defined my primary CTA (what I want people to do)
- [ ] Completed `Templates/profile_card_template.md`

---

## STAGE 3 — BIO LINK PAGE

- [ ] Chosen my platform (Linktree / Carrd / Notion / Other)
- [ ] Written my bio page headline (not just my job title)
- [ ] Arranged my links in priority order (CTA first)
- [ ] Have a profile photo ready (professional, clear background)
- [ ] Applied brand colors
- [ ] Tested on mobile

---

## STAGE 4 — LINKEDIN OPTIMIZATION

- [ ] Updated profile photo (professional headshot)
- [ ] Created or updated LinkedIn banner with brand colors
- [ ] Rewrote headline using outcome formula (not just job title)
- [ ] Updated About section using short bio + bullet points + CTA
- [ ] Added at least 3 items to Featured section
- [ ] Updated top 3 experience entries with outcome language
- [ ] Added relevant skills (top 5 minimum)

---

## STAGE 5 — BRAND FOUNDATION

- [ ] Completed `Templates/personal_branding_structure.md`
- [ ] Defined 3 content pillars
- [ ] Written my brand statement (I help X achieve Y through Z)
- [ ] Noted my tone characteristics
- [ ] Listed words I use and words I avoid
- [ ] Checked `Resources/brand_assets_checklist.md` — noted missing assets

---

## STAGE 6 — SEND FOR GUIDED SETUP

- [ ] Exported / saved my completed content form
- [ ] Read `Consultation/next_steps.md` for submission instructions
- [ ] Sent content form to ZeroOrigins team
- [ ] Booked support session if needed (link in next_steps.md)

---

## STAGE 7 — LAUNCH

- [ ] Bio link page live and tested
- [ ] LinkedIn profile updated and reviewed
- [ ] All profile platforms consistent (same photo, same bio, same CTA)
- [ ] Shared new profile with at least 3 people for feedback
- [ ] Set a 30-day content commitment (e.g., 3 posts/week)

---

## POST-LAUNCH REVIEW (30 days after setup)

- [ ] Checked LinkedIn profile views and connection requests
- [ ] Reviewed which links on bio page are getting clicks
- [ ] Updated copy based on feedback received
- [ ] Booked a 30-day check-in call with ZeroOrigins if needed

---

*A complete digital identity is one where every platform says the same thing,
in the same tone, pointing to the same CTA. That is the goal.*
