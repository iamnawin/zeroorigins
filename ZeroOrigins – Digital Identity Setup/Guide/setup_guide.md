# Digital Identity Setup Guide
**ZeroOrigins – Digital Identity Setup**

This guide walks you through every step of building a clean,
professional digital identity. Follow it in sequence.

---

## PHASE 1 — GATHER YOUR CONTENT

**Estimated time: 30–45 minutes**

Before touching any design tool or platform, collect all your raw material.

### Step 1.1 — Fill the content form
Open `Templates/content_collection_form.md` and answer every question.
This is your source of truth. All copy comes from this.

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Skipping the "what makes you different" section — this is your positioning
- Using job title language instead of outcome language
  - Bad: "I am a marketing manager"
  - Good: "I help e-commerce brands turn email lists into repeat buyers"
- Being too humble about your results — use numbers when you have them

### Step 1.2 — Gather existing assets
Check what you already have:
- [ ] Professional headshot (high resolution)
- [ ] Logo or wordmark (PNG + SVG ideally)
- [ ] Brand colors (hex codes)
- [ ] Existing bio copy (LinkedIn, website)
- [ ] Portfolio links or case study URLs
- [ ] Testimonials or client quotes

If you are missing any of these, note them — we will help you create or source them.

---

## PHASE 2 — BUILD YOUR PROFILE CARD

**Estimated time: 20–30 minutes**

### Step 2.1 — Write your one-line bio
This is the hardest sentence you will write. Do it first.

**Formula:** [WHO YOU ARE] who helps [TARGET AUDIENCE] [ACHIEVE OUTCOME].

**Test it:** Read it aloud. If it sounds like a corporate press release, rewrite it.
It should sound like how you would introduce yourself at dinner.

### Step 2.2 — Write your short bio (3–4 sentences)
Use your one-liner as sentence one.
Add a credibility signal in sentence two.
Add a proof point or result in sentence three.
Close with what you are working on or your CTA.

### Step 2.3 — Extend to long bio
Take your short bio and add:
- Your origin story (why you started doing this)
- Your method or approach (what makes your process different)
- A second proof point
- A personal detail (makes you human, not a robot)

### Step 2.4 — Fill the profile card template
Open `Templates/profile_card_template.md` and transfer your copy.
This becomes your master document — reuse it across all platforms.

## PHASE 3 — SET UP YOUR BIO LINK PAGE

**Estimated time: 30–60 minutes**

### Step 3.1 — Choose your platform
See the platform comparison in `Templates/bio_link_page_template.md`.
Recommendation for most people: **Linktree** (fastest) or **Carrd** (most professional).

### Step 3.2 — Structure your links
Follow this order:
1. Primary CTA (your most important action)
2. Portfolio or results page
3. Lead magnet or free resource
4. Latest content (article, video, post)
5. Contact / Social profiles

### Step 3.3 — Write your headline
Do not use your job title as your headline.
Use your one-line bio or a benefit-focused statement.

### Step 3.4 — Add your photo
Use a professional headshot. Not a travel photo. Not a logo.
People connect with faces — use yours.

### Step 3.5 — Apply brand colors and fonts
Keep it simple: one primary color, one neutral, max two fonts.

## PHASE 4 — OPTIMIZE YOUR LINKEDIN PROFILE

**Estimated time: 45–90 minutes**

LinkedIn is the highest-ROI platform for B2B digital identity.

### Step 4.1 — Profile photo and banner
- Photo: professional, high contrast background, face fills 60% of frame
- Banner: use your brand colors, add a tagline or CTA

### Step 4.2 — Headline (the most important field)
Do not just put your job title.
**Formula:** [Title] | [What I do for whom] | [Outcome or proof]
Example: AI Consultant | Helping SaaS teams automate ops | 50+ workflows delivered

### Step 4.3 — About section
Use your short bio from the profile card.
Add 3–5 bullet points of specific results.
End with a clear CTA and your contact info.

### Step 4.4 — Featured section
Add your top 3–5 links:
- Your bio link page
- Best case study or portfolio piece
- Lead magnet
- Top LinkedIn post (once you have one)

### Step 4.5 — Experience section
For each role: describe what you did in outcome language.
Not: "Managed marketing campaigns"
But: "Ran email campaigns that generated ₹40L in pipeline in 6 months"

## PHASE 5 — ESTABLISH YOUR CONTENT PRESENCE

**Estimated time: Ongoing**

### Step 5.1 — Define your content pillars
Use `Templates/personal_branding_structure.md` Part 3.
Pick 3 topics. Commit to them for 90 days before adding more.

### Step 5.2 — Create a content template
Every post you write should have:
- Hook (first line stops the scroll)
- Body (insight, story, or list)
- CTA (what to do next)

### Step 5.3 — Post consistently, not constantly
3 posts per week beats 1 post per day that burns you out.
Consistency over frequency.

### Step 5.4 — Engage before you post
Spend 15 minutes commenting on others' posts before writing your own.
This warms up the algorithm and builds genuine connections.

## PHASE 6 — REVIEW AND SEND

Once you have completed Phases 1–3:

1. Re-read everything as if you are a new potential client
2. Ask: Does this make me want to reach out?
3. Fix anything that feels vague or generic
4. Send your completed content form to us for review

See `Consultation/next_steps.md` for how to send and what happens next.
