# IgnAIte — Solo Founder Starter Plan

## Goal
Stop wasting time in planning loops and start using n8n for one real revenue path.

## Right Now: What To Start With
Start **IgnAIte** as an **AI automation and build service**, not as a full course business yet.

### First offer to sell
**IgnAIte Automation Setup**

Help small businesses, creators, institutes, or founders with:
- lead capture automation
- email / WhatsApp follow-up
- CRM or Google Sheet sync
- reminders and notifications
- simple reporting workflows

This is the easiest offer to:
- explain
- demo
- sell
- deliver
- convert into a case study

---

## Rule For The Next 14 Days
Do not build:
- 10 workflows
- full academy
- heavy branding changes
- multi-agent architecture
- complicated outreach engines

Build **one workflow**, **one demo**, and **one offer**.

## First n8n Workflow To Build

### Workflow Name
**Lead Capture → Follow-up Automation**

### Flow
1. Form submission or webhook trigger
2. Save lead to Google Sheet / Airtable / database
3. Send email confirmation to lead
4. Send internal notification to you
5. Add follow-up reminder after X hours or days

### Why this workflow first
Because every business understands it.
It is simple, useful, and sellable.

---

## What To Use n8n For First
Only these:

### Workflow 1 — Lead Intake
- webhook
- save lead
- internal notification

### Workflow 2 — Follow-up Reminder
- if lead not updated, remind you
- next action prompt

### Workflow 3 — Weekly Summary
- leads collected
- demos booked
- pending follow-ups

### Workflow 4 — Content Support (optional later)
- idea capture
- content draft packaging

## What Not To Do In n8n Right Now

- multi-agent memory systems
- giant workflow chains
- full cold email automation
- social auto-posting to everything
- complex AI orchestration

That comes later.
Right now you need **proof**, not workflow fireworks.
