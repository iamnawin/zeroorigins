# IgnAIte — The Offer

## What Is This

IgnAIte Automation Setup is a done-for-you AI automation service.
We build the workflows that run your business in the background — so you stop doing repetitive work manually.

---

## What The Client Gets

- A fully working automation workflow tailored to their business
- Connected to their existing tools (Google Sheets, WhatsApp, email, CRM, or Airtable)
- A 1-hour handoff session with a walkthrough of every step
- A simple guide so they can monitor and manage it themselves
- Optional: 2-week post-setup support window

---

## Tools We Use

- **n8n** — workflow automation (self-hosted or cloud)
- **Google Sheets / Airtable** — data storage and tracking
- **Gmail / SMTP** — automated email
- **WhatsApp Business API or Twilio** — messaging automation
- **Webhooks / Typeform / Tally** — form triggers
- **Notion / Slack** — internal alerts and reporting
