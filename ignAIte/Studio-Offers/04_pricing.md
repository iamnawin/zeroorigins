# IgnAIte — Pricing

> All prices are in INR. Adjust based on client size and complexity.
> Start with Starter for the first 1–2 pilot clients.

---

## Starter — ₹8,000 – ₹12,000

**Best for:** Solo founders, small businesses, single-use-case automation

**Includes:**
- 1 workflow (e.g., lead capture + auto email + Google Sheet entry)
- Up to 2 integrations (e.g., Tally form + Gmail + Google Sheets)
- 1 handoff session (60 min)
- Basic documentation
- 1 round of revisions

**Delivery:** 3–5 days

---

## Standard — ₹18,000 – ₹28,000

**Best for:** Coaching institutes, consultants, growing small businesses

**Includes:**
- 2–3 workflows (lead intake + follow-up + reporting)
- Up to 4 integrations
- Lead routing and tagging logic
- Internal notification system (Slack or WhatsApp)
- 1 handoff session (90 min)
- Full documentation
- 2 weeks post-setup support (async)
- 2 rounds of revisions

**Delivery:** 5–7 days

---

## Custom — ₹40,000+

**Best for:** Founders, agencies, businesses with complex or multi-step needs

**Includes:**
- Everything in Standard
- Multi-step workflows with conditions and branches
- CRM or database integration
- AI layer (GPT for lead scoring, response generation, or classification)
- Custom triggers and error handling
- Priority delivery
- 30-day support window
- Monthly check-in (optional add-on)

**Delivery:** 7–14 days (scoped per project)
