# IgnAIte — Who Is This For

## Primary Audience

People who are running a real business or project, getting leads or inquiries, but losing them because there's no system.

---

## Target Segments

### Small Business Owners
- Retail shops, clinics, salons, gyms, restaurants
- Pain: Inquiries come in from Instagram / WhatsApp but nobody follows up consistently
- Win: Every inquiry is captured, acknowledged, and followed up automatically

### Coaching Institutes & Tutors
- Education centers, skill academies, solo tutors
- Pain: Students enquire on WhatsApp, Excel sheets are a mess, reminders are forgotten
- Win: Lead form → auto confirmation → reminder → admission status tracking

### Consultants & Freelancers
- Business consultants, finance advisors, designers, lawyers
- Pain: Following up with prospects takes time they don't have
- Win: Auto follow-up sequences, meeting reminders, client onboarding flows

### Creators & Personal Brands
- Course creators, YouTubers, coaches selling digital products
- Pain: Lead magnets dump emails into nothing, no nurture sequence
- Win: Lead captured → tagged by interest → email/WhatsApp sequence triggered

### Founders & Early-Stage Startups
- Pre-revenue or early revenue, small team
- Pain: No ops team, every admin task is manual
- Win: Lead intake, reporting, follow-up, and status tracking — all automated

### Local Service Businesses
- Plumbers, electricians, pest control, packers & movers, event planners
- Pain: Enquiries come in at odd hours, no system to respond fast
- Win: Instant auto-reply, lead logged, owner alerted immediately

---

## Who It Is NOT For

- Enterprises needing complex IT procurement
- Businesses with no incoming leads at all
- People who want to build it themselves (that is a different product)
