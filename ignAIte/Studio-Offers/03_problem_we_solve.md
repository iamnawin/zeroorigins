# IgnAIte — The Problem We Solve

## The Core Problem

Most small businesses and founders are generating interest — but converting almost none of it.
Not because the offer is bad. Because there is no system.

---

## The 5 Problems We Fix

### 1. Leads Get Missed
Inquiries come in through WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, forms, calls, and emails.
They live in 5 different places. Nobody tracks them. Half are forgotten.

**What we build:** A single intake point that captures every lead and stores it automatically.

---

### 2. Follow-Up Is Manual (And Doesn't Happen)
The follow-up plan is: "I'll message them later."
Later never comes. The lead goes cold. The sale is lost.

**What we build:** Automatic follow-up sequences triggered the moment a lead comes in. No human needed to start the chain.

---

### 3. Repetitive Tasks Eat Hours Every Week
Sending the same confirmation message, updating the same spreadsheet, creating the same reminder — manually, every single day.

**What we build:** Workflows that do these tasks the moment a trigger fires. Zero manual effort after setup.

---

### 4. Inquiries Are Scattered Across Tools
WhatsApp here, email there, a form somewhere else, notes in a phone.
No unified view. No idea what is happening with any lead.

**What we build:** All leads flow into one place — Google Sheet, Airtable, or CRM — tagged, organized, and visible.

---

### 5. No System For Converting Interested People Into Clients
The business depends on the owner's memory and energy.
When either runs low, conversions drop.

**What we build:** A conversion flow — inquiry → acknowledgment → follow-up → reminder → status update — that runs without the owner touching it.

---

## The Real Cost Of Not Fixing This

- Lost leads = lost revenue (estimate: 30–60% of inquiries never get a response)
- Owner time wasted on admin instead of delivery
- Inconsistent follow-up = unprofessional impression
- No data = no improvement possible

---

## What Changes After

- Every lead is captured
- Every lead gets a response within seconds
- Follow-ups happen on schedule
- Owner gets a weekly summary of what is happening
- The business runs even when the owner is busy
