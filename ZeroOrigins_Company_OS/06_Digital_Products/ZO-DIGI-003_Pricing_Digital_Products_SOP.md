# ZO-DIGI-003 — Pricing Digital Products SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To set prices for digital products that reflect value, convert well, and leave room to raise prices as social proof builds.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins digital products sold via Gumroad or direct link.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Before listing any new digital product.

---

## 5. Pricing Philosophy

- **Price on value, not time spent.** A 2-hour template that saves someone 10 hours is worth $99, not $20.
- **Start in the lower half of your range, raise after 10 sales.** Early buyers get a deal; later buyers pay the real price.
- **Never underprice below $9.** Anything below $9 is not taken seriously.
- **Test "name your price" only for lead magnets.** Not for real products.

---

## 6. Price Points by Product Type

| Product Type | Launch Price | Post-10-Sale Price |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Single template / prompt pack | $9–$19 | $19–$29 |
| Workflow JSON pack (3–5 workflows) | $29–$49 | $49–$79 |
| Mini-course (1–3 hours) | $49–$79 | $79–$149 |
| Full toolkit (templates + guide + workflow) | $79–$149 | $149–$299 |
| Done-with-you program | $299–$499 | $499–$799 |

---

## 7. Pricing Tiers on Gumroad

Use Gumroad's tiered pricing to offer:

| Tier | What's Included | Price |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Basic | Core product | Launch price |
| Pro | Core + bonus pack | 1.5x basic |
| VIP | Core + bonus + 30-min call | 3x basic |

Not every product needs all tiers. A simple template only needs one tier.

---

## 8. Discount Policy

- **No blanket discounts.** Discounting trains buyers to wait for sales.
- **Allowed:** Early-bird pricing (first 10 buyers get $X off) — set a hard deadline.
- **Bundle discount:** 20–30% off when buying 2+ products together.
- **Not allowed:** Responding to "can I get a discount?" with yes. Politely decline: "The price is set to reflect the value inside."

---

## 9. Price Raise Trigger

Raise price after:
- 10 sales at launch price, OR
- 30 days, whichever comes first

Raise by 20–30%. Notify your list before raising: "Price goes up [date]."

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Price is above $9
- [ ] Price reflects value, not time spent
- [ ] Gumroad listing shows what buyer gets (not just what it is)
- [ ] Price-raise trigger is set (calendar reminder)

---

## 11. Approval Required

Founder decision.

---

## 12. Output

- Finalized price on Gumroad listing
- Price-raise calendar reminder set

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / Digital / [ProductName] / Pricing_Notes.md`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Changing price too frequently** — buyers talk; inconsistent pricing looks unprofessional
- **Setting price based on what competitors charge** — price on your delivery and your buyer's outcome

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Per product launch; revisit quarterly.
