# ZO-DIGI-001 — Digital Product Strategy SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To define what digital products ZeroOrigins builds, why, and how they fit the overall revenue strategy.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins digital product lines: templates, toolkits, mini-courses, workflow packs.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Before starting any new digital product build.

---

## 5. Why Digital Products

Digital products serve three purposes for ZeroOrigins:

1. **Passive revenue** — income that doesn't require client work
2. **Proof of expertise** — published products establish authority
3. **Lead magnet pipeline** — product buyers become service clients

---

## 6. ZeroOrigins Digital Product Lines

| Product Type | Examples | Pricing Range | Effort |
|-------------|----------|---------------|--------|
| Workflow Starter Pack | n8n templates, JSON exports | $9–$49 | Low |
| Prompt Libraries | Tested Claude/ChatGPT prompts | $19–$79 | Low |
| Mini-Course / Workshop | 1–3 hour video + notes | $49–$199 | Medium |
| Full System / Toolkit | Templates + workflows + guide | $99–$499 | High |
| Done-With-You Program | Templates + 2 live calls | $299–$799 | High |

---

## 7. Product Selection Criteria

Before building, score the idea:

| Question | Yes = 1 | No = 0 |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Do I already know how to do this? | 1 | 0 |
| Can I build it in under 5 days? | 1 | 0 |
| Is there a clear buyer (who + why)? | 1 | 0 |
| Can I sell it without 1:1 explanation? | 1 | 0 |
| Is this productizing existing client work? | 1 | 0 |

Score 4+: Build it. Score 3 or below: Rethink.

---

## 8. Revenue Model

- **Primary platform:** Gumroad (see ZO-DIGI-002)
- **Secondary:** Lemon Squeezy (for subscription bundles)
- **Pricing strategy:** Start at bottom of range, raise after 10 sales
- **Bundles:** Combine 2–3 products into a bundle at 30% discount on combined price

---

## 9. Product Naming Convention

`[Brand] — [Outcome] [Type]`

Examples:
- ZeroOrigins — LeadFlow Starter Kit
- ZeroOrigins — 50 Claude Prompts for Founders
- ZeroOrigins — n8n Automation Starter Pack

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Product scores 4+ on selection criteria
- [ ] Clear target buyer defined
- [ ] Priced within range for product type
- [ ] Listed on Gumroad before building marketing

---

## 11. Approval Required

Founder go/no-go before build.

---

## 12. Output

- Product concept doc (1 page)
- Gumroad draft listing

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / Digital / [ProductName] /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Building without a clear buyer** — "everyone" is not a buyer
- **Underpricing** — $9 templates signal low quality; $29 signals more value

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Quarterly product portfolio review.
