# ZO-DIGI-007 — Reviews and Testimonials SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To systematically collect, store, and use buyer reviews and testimonials to build trust and improve conversion on product listings.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins digital products.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

- 7 days after every purchase (automated ask)
- Any time a buyer sends unsolicited positive feedback

---

## 5. When to Ask

| Timing | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Day 7 post-purchase | Email (automated or manual) |
| After buyer DMs you something positive | Immediate reply asking permission to quote |
| After a successful call or walkthrough | Ask at end of call |

Never ask for a review within 24 hours of purchase — they haven't used it yet.

---

## 6. Review Request Email Template

```
Subject: Quick favour — 2 minutes

Hi [Name],

It's been a week since you picked up [Product Name].

If it's been useful, would you mind leaving a quick review on Gumroad? Even 1–2 sentences helps other people decide if it's right for them.

[Gumroad review link]

If something didn't work for you, I'd rather know directly — just reply here.

Thanks,
Naveen
```

---

## 7. Collecting Unsolicited Testimonials

When a buyer says something positive via email, LinkedIn DM, or WhatsApp:

1. Reply: "Really glad to hear that! Would you mind if I quoted this on the product page?"
2. If yes: save the quote with their name and role (e.g., "Priya S., solo consultant, Bangalore")
3. If they want anonymity: "Priya S." or "Marketing consultant, Mumbai" is fine

---

## 8. Testimonial Storage Format

Save in Google Drive → `Products / Digital / [ProductName] / Testimonials /`

One file: `Testimonials_[ProductName].md`

Format per entry:
```
---
Name: [First name, last initial]
Role/City: [e.g., Freelance designer, Pune]
Date: [Month Year]
Source: [Gumroad / Email / LinkedIn DM]
Quote: "[Their exact words]"
Permission to use: [Yes / Anonymized]
---
```

---

## 9. How to Use Testimonials

| Where | How |
|-------|-----|
| Gumroad listing | Add 2–3 testimonials in the description (text only) |
| LinkedIn post | Quote with permission as social proof |
| Email campaigns | Use in promotional emails |
| Proposals | Use if relevant to service work |

**Format for Gumroad:** Add a "What buyers are saying" section at the bottom of the product description:

```
## What Buyers Are Saying

"[Quote]"
— [Name], [Role/City]

"[Quote]"
— [Name], [Role/City]
```

---

## 10. Handling Negative Reviews

If a buyer leaves a negative Gumroad review:
1. Reply publicly — professionally, within 24 hours
2. Offer to fix the issue or refund
3. Do not argue; do not delete (Gumroad doesn't allow it anyway)
4. Use the feedback to improve the product

Template reply:
```
"Thank you for the feedback. I'm sorry this didn't land the way I intended. I'd love to make it right — please email me directly at naveen.naidu21@gmail.com and I'll sort it out."
```

---

## 11. Quality Checklist

- [ ] 7-day review request email is active (scheduled or templated)
- [ ] At least 2 testimonials on product page before launch
- [ ] Testimonials stored in product folder with permission noted
- [ ] Negative reviews responded to within 24 hours

---

## 12. Approval Required

None — Founder manages directly.

---

## 13. Output

- Testimonials file in Google Drive
- Updated Gumroad description with buyer quotes

---

## 14. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / Digital / [ProductName] / Testimonials /`

---

## 15. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Waiting too long to ask** — ask at day 7, not day 30; motivation to respond fades
- **Using a quote without permission** — always get written confirmation (email reply is enough)
- **Ignoring negative reviews** — public silence looks worse than a bad review itself

---

## 16. Review Frequency

Monthly — check if new reviews need to be added to listing.
