# ZO-DIGI-005 — Affiliate and Referral SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To grow digital product sales through affiliate partners and buyer referrals without discounting the core product.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins digital products with 10+ sales already (don't launch an affiliate program with no social proof).

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

After a product reaches 10 sales. At that point, activate affiliate program.

---

## 5. Affiliate Program Setup (Gumroad)

Gumroad has built-in affiliate management.

**Settings:**
- Commission rate: **30%** for all affiliates
- Cookie duration: 30 days
- Payout: After 30-day refund window clears

**How to add an affiliate:**
1. Gumroad → Product → Affiliates → Add affiliate
2. Enter their email
3. Set commission %
4. Send them the tracking link

---

## 6. Who to Recruit as Affiliates

Priority order:

| Tier | Who | Why |
|------|-----|-----|
| 1 | Past buyers who loved the product | They've used it; authentic promoters |
| 2 | Newsletter writers in your niche | Their audience already trusts them |
| 3 | Peers / complementary service providers | They can bundle with their offers |
| 4 | LinkedIn creators with <5K followers | Micro-influencers convert better |

Do NOT recruit affiliates with no niche fit — irrelevant audiences convert at near zero.

---

## 7. Affiliate Outreach Template

```
Subject: Partner on [Product Name]? (30% commission)

Hi [Name],

I saw your [post/content] about [topic] — great stuff.

I run ZeroOrigins and just launched [Product Name], which helps [target buyer] [outcome in one line].

Given your audience, I think it'd be a natural fit. I'd love to offer you a 30% affiliate commission on every sale.

No pressure to post — only share if you think it genuinely helps your people.

Want me to send your affiliate link?

— Naveen
```

---

## 8. Affiliate Support Kit

When an affiliate says yes, send them:

- [ ] Their unique Gumroad affiliate link
- [ ] 2–3 pre-written LinkedIn/Twitter posts they can adapt
- [ ] The product thumbnail (1200x630px)
- [ ] 3 bullet points on what makes it worth recommending
- [ ] Your email for questions

---

## 9. Referral Program (Buyer Referrals)

After purchase, include in the welcome email:

```
Know someone who'd find this useful?
Share this link and earn 30% of their purchase:
[Affiliate link — set this up per buyer in Gumroad]
```

This turns buyers into advocates automatically.

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Product has 10+ sales before activating affiliates
- [ ] Gumroad affiliate commission set to 30%
- [ ] At least 3 affiliates recruited in first month
- [ ] Affiliate support kit sent to every affiliate

---

## 11. Approval Required

None — Founder manages directly.

---

## 12. Output

- Active affiliate list in Gumroad
- Affiliate kit saved to Drive

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / Digital / [ProductName] / Affiliates /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Activating affiliates before product has social proof** — affiliates need to believe in what they're promoting; no reviews = low motivation
- **Not sending the affiliate kit** — affiliates who have to write their own copy rarely promote

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Monthly — check affiliate performance, remove inactive ones after 60 days.
