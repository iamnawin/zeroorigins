## Week 1 — Entity Setup and Name Finalization

**Goal:** Lock in your legal identity before doing anything else.

- [ ] Decide on entity type: **Private Limited Company** is recommended for scalability, investor-readiness, and credibility. LLP is simpler if you want lower compliance. Proprietorship is fastest but weakest. Consult CA/CS to confirm.
- [ ] Finalize your company name (e.g., ZeroOrigins Technologies Private Limited)
- [ ] Check name availability on **MCA (mca.gov.in)** — search for similar names
- [ ] Check trademark availability on **IP India (ipindia.gov.in)**
- [ ] Buy your domain: zeroorigins.ai or zeroorigins.in (whichever is available)
- [ ] Secure social handles: LinkedIn Company Page, Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube
- [ ] Prepare founder documents:
  - [ ] PAN card
  - [ ] Aadhaar card
  - [ ] Passport-size photo
  - [ ] Address proof (utility bill or bank statement)
- [ ] Create a Google Workspace account (founder@zeroorigins.ai or similar)
- [ ] Create a shared admin folder in Google Drive: `ZeroOrigins / Admin / Incorporation`
- [ ] Book a meeting with a CA or CS to initiate incorporation

**SOP to read:** `ZO-COMP-001_Company_Registration_SOP.md`

## Week 2 — Incorporation and Banking

**Goal:** Start the legal registration process and prepare for banking.

- [ ] Apply for DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) — required for MCA filing
- [ ] Director Identification Number (DIN) — obtained during MCA filing
- [ ] Submit SPICe+ form on MCA portal (your CA/CS will do this)
- [ ] Prepare Memorandum of Association (MoA) — include all business objects
- [ ] Prepare Articles of Association (AoA)
- [ ] Agree on founder shareholding split (if co-founders)
- [ ] After CIN is received, apply for business bank account:
  - [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
  - [ ] MoA and AoA
  - [ ] Board resolution for bank account opening
  - [ ] PAN card of company
  - [ ] Director KYC documents
- [ ] Apply for GST registration (mandatory above Rs. 20L turnover, recommended from day 1 for B2B)

**SOPs to read:**
- `ZO-COMP-002_MCA_and_Corporate_Records_SOP.md`
- `ZO-COMP-003_GST_Registration_and_Filing_SOP.md`

## Week 4 — Launch and Outreach

**Goal:** Get in front of potential clients and make your first sale.

- [ ] Launch website with a clear CTA (book a call, or buy the starter kit)
- [ ] Publish first LinkedIn post about what ZeroOrigins does
- [ ] Create your first Gumroad product (Workflow Starter Kit — $49 or equivalent)
- [ ] Start outreach: identify 20 target businesses on LinkedIn
- [ ] Run your first 3 discovery calls
- [ ] Capture all leads in the Lead Tracker
- [ ] Send at least 2 proposals
- [ ] Follow up with everyone who did not respond within 5 days

**SOPs to read:**
- `ZO-SALES-001_Lead_Capture_SOP.md`
- `ZO-MKT-003_LinkedIn_Post_SOP.md`

## After Week 4 — Ongoing Operations

Once the foundation is in place:

- Run weekly: review Lead Tracker, Invoice Tracker, Content Calendar
- Run monthly: close accounting, reconcile invoices, post GST (if applicable)
- Run quarterly: review all SOPs, update pricing, plan next product or campaign
- Run annually: file ROC returns, ITR, renew domains, review compliance calendar
