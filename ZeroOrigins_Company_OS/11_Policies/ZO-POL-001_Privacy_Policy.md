## 2. What Data We Collect

### From Clients and Prospects

- Name, email address, phone number
- Company name, job title
- Project requirements and business information shared during discovery or delivery
- Payment information (processed via Razorpay / Stripe — we do not store card numbers)
- Communications via email, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn

### From Website Visitors

- Analytics data (page views, referral source, device type) via tools like Google Analytics
- Contact form submissions
- IP address (collected automatically by hosting infrastructure)

### From Digital Product Buyers (Gumroad)

- Email address provided at purchase (managed by Gumroad)
- We receive only the email and product purchased — Gumroad handles payment data

## 3. How We Use Data

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Deliver services and projects | Contract performance |
| Send invoices and receipts | Legal obligation |
| Follow up on proposals and enquiries | Legitimate interest |
| Send product updates to buyers | Legitimate interest / consent |
| Improve services and workflows | Legitimate interest |
| Comply with tax and legal requirements | Legal obligation |

We do **not** sell client or prospect data to third parties.  
We do **not** use client data to train AI models without explicit written consent.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Client project files are stored in Google Drive (ZeroOrigins workspace)
- Credentials and API keys are stored in a password manager (see ZO-SEC-001)
- Automation workflows in n8n do not persist sensitive client data beyond what is needed for execution
- All team members and contractors must sign an NDA before accessing client data

---

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Client project files | 3 years after project close |
| Invoices and financial records | 7 years (tax requirement) |
| Prospect/lead contact data | 2 years from last contact |
| Website analytics | Rolling 26 months (Google Analytics default) |

After the retention period, data is deleted or anonymised.
