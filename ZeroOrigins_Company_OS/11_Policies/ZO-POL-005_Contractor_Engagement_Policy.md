# ZO-POL-005 — Contractor Engagement Policy

**ID:** ZO-POL-005  
**Department:** Operations / HR  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Effective Date:** May 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

Define how ZeroOrigins engages, manages, and pays freelancers and contractors — to keep engagements clean, protect IP, and avoid compliance issues.

---

## 2. When to Use a Contractor

Use contractors for:
- Overflow project work (extra n8n builds, frontend dev, design)
- Specialist tasks not in the founder's skillset (legal, CA, video editing)
- Specific project phases with a clear end date

Do **not** use contractors as de-facto permanent employees — if work is ongoing and integral to operations, evaluate whether a formal hire is needed.

---

## 3. Before Any Work Starts

The following must be completed before a contractor starts:

- [ ] Scope of work agreed in writing (email is minimum, formal contract preferred)
- [ ] Rate and payment terms confirmed
- [ ] NDA signed (use ZO-TMPL-004_NDA_Template.md)
- [ ] Contractor understands the IP policy (ZO-POL-003) — all work product belongs to ZeroOrigins
- [ ] Access provisioned only to tools and files needed for the task (follow ZO-SEC-001)
- [ ] Contractor's PAN (for Indian contractors) or equivalent collected for tax records

---

## 4. Contractor Payment Terms

| Scenario | Payment Terms |
|----------|---------------|
| Small task (under Rs. 5,000 / $100) | 100% on delivery |
| Medium project (Rs. 5,000–50,000) | 50% upfront, 50% on delivery |
| Large project (above Rs. 50,000) | 30% upfront, 40% at milestone, 30% on final delivery |
| Retainer / ongoing | Monthly, within 5 business days of month end |

All payments via bank transfer (preferred), UPI, or Razorpay. Request an invoice from the contractor before payment.

---

## 5. Communication Standards

- Use email or WhatsApp for task communication — keep it professional and documented
- Set clear deadlines in writing at the start of each task
- Do not give contractors access to client credentials unless strictly necessary
- All client-facing communications go through the ZeroOrigins founder only — contractors do not represent ZeroOrigins to clients directly

---

## 6. Quality and Delivery

- Contractors are responsible for delivering work that meets the agreed specification
- Revision rounds: up to 2 revision rounds are included in the agreed rate unless otherwise specified
- If a contractor misses a deadline without prior communication, ZeroOrigins reserves the right to reduce the fee proportionally or cancel the engagement

---

## 7. Offboarding

When a contractor's engagement ends:
- Remove all access immediately (follow ZO-SEC-004_Access_Revocation_SOP.md)
- Confirm all deliverables have been handed over
- Collect any company data stored on their personal devices
- Issue final payment within 5 business days of final delivery confirmation
- Retain contractor NDA and agreement on file for 3 years

---

## 8. Compliance (India)

- For Indian contractors paid above Rs. 30,000 per transaction, TDS at 10% may apply under Section 194J (professional services)
- Consult CA for current thresholds and rules — do not make assumptions
- Collect PAN before making payments above Rs. 20,000 total in a financial year
