# ZO-POL-004 — Refund and Cancellation Policy

**ID:** ZO-POL-004  
**Department:** Finance / Legal  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Effective Date:** May 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

Define the conditions under which refunds or project cancellations are accepted, to protect both ZeroOrigins and clients.

---

## 2. Service Projects (Custom Automation / AI Systems)

### Deposits and Upfront Payments
- Deposits (typically 50% of project value) are **non-refundable** once work has begun.
- If the client cancels before any work has started, a full refund of the deposit is at the founder's discretion (case-by-case).

### Mid-Project Cancellation by Client
- If a client cancels after work has started, they owe payment for all work completed to that point.
- ZeroOrigins will issue a final invoice for work done, calculated as a pro-rata share of the total project value.
- Any deposit already paid is applied against this invoice.

### Mid-Project Cancellation by ZeroOrigins
- If ZeroOrigins cancels a project (only in cases of scope abuse, non-payment, or breach of contract), the client will be refunded for any prepaid work not yet delivered.

### Scope Disputes
- If a dispute arises over deliverables, both parties agree to a 14-day resolution window before any refund or escalation process begins.
- See ZO-AUTO-002 for scope documentation and ZO-TMPL-003 for contract dispute clauses.

---

## 3. Digital Products (Gumroad)

### Standard Policy
- All digital product sales are **final**. No refunds on templates, kits, or guides once downloaded.

### Exception: Product Does Not Work as Described
- If a product has a material defect (broken files, incorrect content, does not match the description), a full refund will be issued within 7 days of purchase.
- Client must email naveen.naidu21@gmail.com with the order reference and description of the issue.

### Exception: Duplicate Purchase
- If a customer accidentally purchased the same product twice, the duplicate will be refunded within 3 business days upon request.

---

## 4. Subscription or Retainer Services

- Clients on monthly retainers may cancel with **30 days' written notice**.
- No refunds for the current billing period — the service continues until the notice period expires.
- If ZeroOrigins ceases a retainer (non-payment, scope abuse), the client is notified in writing and the retainer ends at the next billing cycle.

---

## 5. How to Request a Refund

1. Email naveen.naidu21@gmail.com with the subject: **"Refund Request — [Invoice/Order ID]"**
2. Include the reason for the request
3. ZeroOrigins will respond within 3 business days
4. Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days via the original payment method

---

## 6. Chargebacks and Disputes

If a client raises a chargeback with their bank without contacting ZeroOrigins first:
- ZeroOrigins reserves the right to dispute the chargeback with documented proof of delivery.
- Future work with that client may be suspended pending resolution.
