# ZO-MKT-004 — Email Marketing SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To build and use an email list as a direct line to potential clients and product buyers — without depending on platform algorithms.

---

## 2. Scope

ZeroOrigins email newsletter and promotional campaigns.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Weekly for newsletter sends; as-needed for product launches and offers.

---

## 5. Email Stack

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) | Primary email tool — newsletters, sequences, automations |
| Gumroad | Buyer list management (buyers only) |
| Google Sheets | Manual subscriber tracking if needed |

---

## 6. List Segmentation

| Segment | Who's In It | What to Send |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Main list | All subscribers | Weekly newsletter |
| Buyers | Anyone who purchased a product | Product updates, upsells |
| Leads | People who booked a call or filled a form | Follow-up sequences |
| Inactive (90+ days no open) | Subscribers who haven't opened in 90 days | Re-engagement campaign, then remove |

---

## 7. Weekly Newsletter Format

Send every Thursday (or pick one day and stick to it).

```
Subject: [One specific thing — not "Newsletter #47"]

[Hook — 1–2 sentences: what you're sharing and why it matters today]

[Body — 300–500 words max]
- One main idea
- One real example or story
- Practical takeaway the reader can use this week

[One CTA — link to product, service, or next step]

[Sign-off]
— Naveen, ZeroOrigins
[Unsubscribe] [Update preferences]
```

Subject line rules:
- Be specific ("How I automated my invoicing in 2 hours") not generic ("My thoughts on AI")
- Under 50 characters when possible
- No clickbait — if the email doesn't deliver what the subject promises, unsubscribes spike

---

## 8. Launch Email Sequence (3 Emails)

When launching a product or offer:

| Email | When | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| Email 1: "Something's coming" | 5 days before launch | Build anticipation |
| Email 2: "It's live" | Launch day | Drive sales |
| Email 3: "Last chance" | 2–3 days after launch | Urgency close |

**Email 2 (Launch Day) structure:**
1. Here's what it is
2. Here's who it's for
3. Here's what's inside
4. Price + link
5. Short answer to one likely objection

---

## 9. List Growth Methods

| Method | Effort | Effectiveness |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| LinkedIn CTA in bio | Low | Medium |
| Lead magnet (free template) | Medium | High |
| Post CTA ("DM me for the template") | Low | High |
| Gumroad product upsell | Low | Medium |
| Speaking / guest posts | High | High |

Target: grow by 50 subscribers/month minimum.

---

## 10. Email Hygiene

- Remove subscribers inactive for 90+ days (send one re-engagement email first)
- Never buy email lists — violates GDPR, DPDPA, and destroys deliverability
- Use double opt-in to reduce spam complaints
- Check spam score before sending (Brevo has built-in tool)

---

## 11. Metrics to Track

| Metric | Healthy Benchmark |
|--------|------------------|
| Open rate | 30%+ |
| Click rate | 3%+ |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 0.5% per send |
| List growth | +50/month |

---

## 12. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Newsletter sends every week on the same day
- [ ] Subject line is specific and under 50 characters
- [ ] One CTA per email (not three)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link works (test before every send)
- [ ] Inactive subscribers cleaned quarterly

---

## 13. Approval Required

None — Founder manages directly.

---

## 14. Output

- Weekly newsletter sent
- Launch sequences triggered on time
- List health metrics logged monthly

---

## 15. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Marketing / Email / `
- Store all sent email copy as `Email_[Date]_[Subject].md`

---

## 16. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Inconsistent sending** — the list goes cold; subscribers forget who you are
- **Buying lists** — illegal under DPDPA; destroys deliverability; wastes money
- **Too many CTAs** — one email, one ask; multiple CTAs confuse and reduce clicks

---

## 17. Review Frequency

Monthly metrics review; quarterly list hygiene.
