# ZO-MKT-003 — LinkedIn Growth SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To grow the ZeroOrigins LinkedIn presence systematically — building an audience of potential clients and buyers through consistent, high-value content.

---

## 2. Scope

ZeroOrigins LinkedIn page and Founder (Naveen) personal LinkedIn profile.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Weekly — every Monday morning, plan the week's posts.

---

## 5. Profile Optimization (One-Time Setup)

Before posting, ensure the profile is set up correctly:

| Element | What It Should Say |
|---------|-------------------|
| Headline | "AI Automation for Founders → ZeroOrigins" (not "Founder at ZeroOrigins") |
| About | Problem you solve → who for → proof → CTA (max 5 short paragraphs) |
| Featured | Pin your best post + one product link + one case study |
| Banner | Simple, branded — product tagline or offer |
| CTA button | "Visit website" or "Book a call" |

---

## 6. Posting Schedule

| Day | Post Type |
|-----|-----------|
| Monday | Educational post (pillar: AI Automation Education) |
| Wednesday | Story or behind-the-scenes (pillar: Process) |
| Friday | Results/case study or product (pillar: Client Results or Offer) |

Minimum: 3 posts/week. Ideal: 5 posts/week (add Tuesday and Thursday with lighter content).

---

## 7. Post Format That Works on LinkedIn

**Text post (most reach):**
```
[Hook — 1 line that stops the scroll]

[1 blank line]

[Body — short paragraphs, max 3 lines each]
[Use line breaks liberally]

[1 blank line]

[CTA — one clear next step]
```

**Carousel (high saves):**
- 6–10 slides
- Slide 1: Hook / Bold claim
- Slides 2–8: One point per slide
- Slide 9: Summary / Key takeaway
- Slide 10: CTA

**Video (builds trust fastest):**
- 60–90 seconds max for feed video
- Captions required (85% watch muted)
- Start mid-action, not with intro

---

## 8. Hook Formulas

The first line is everything — if the hook fails, nothing else matters.

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Number + outcome | "5 automations that saved my client 10 hours/week" |
| Counterintuitive claim | "The cheapest AI tool is doing more work than the most expensive one" |
| Story opener | "My client was manually copying 200 rows of data every Monday morning." |
| Question | "What's stopping you from automating your lead follow-up?" |
| Mistake / failure | "I built the wrong product for 3 months. Here's what I learned." |

---

## 9. Engagement Rules

- Reply to every comment within 24 hours (LinkedIn rewards this with more reach)
- Comment on 5–10 posts from ICP accounts daily (use LinkedIn as a conversation tool, not just a broadcast channel)
- Like and comment before posting — LinkedIn's algorithm rewards active users
- Never use engagement pods (fake engagement gets accounts flagged)

---

## 10. Content That Gets DMs (Leads)

Posts that generate direct sales conversations:
- "I just automated [specific workflow] for a client. Here's the exact setup." (with screenshot or Loom link)
- "We have 2 spots open for [service offer] this month. Here's how it works."
- "This one workflow change made a client $[X] in recovered follow-ups."

These are not "viral" posts — they speak directly to buyers.

## 16. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Posting and ghosting** — not replying to comments kills reach and trust
- **No hook** — starting a post with "I am pleased to share..." gets no impressions
- **Too polished** — corporate-sounding content underperforms on LinkedIn; be human
- **Only broadcasting, never engaging** — engagement on others' content drives profile discovery
