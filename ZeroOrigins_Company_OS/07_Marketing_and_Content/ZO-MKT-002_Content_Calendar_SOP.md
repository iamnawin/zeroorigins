# ZO-MKT-002 — Content Calendar SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To plan, schedule, and execute content consistently across all ZeroOrigins channels — so posting is never a last-minute panic and every week has content that serves a business goal.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins content: LinkedIn posts, email newsletter, YouTube/Loom videos, product launches.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Every Monday morning — plan the week's content. First Monday of the month — plan the full month.

---

## 5. Monthly Content Planning (First Monday)

Spend 45 minutes planning the full month before it starts.

**Step 1: Identify the month's priority**

Each month has one primary marketing goal:

| Month Priority | Example |
|----------------|---------|
| Lead generation | Push discovery call bookings |
| Product launch | Launch a new digital product |
| Nurture/authority | Build trust, no hard sell |
| Re-engagement | Reactivate dormant leads |

Only one priority per month. This determines your 15% promotional content.

**Step 2: Fill the content calendar template**

Use the Monthly Content Calendar table (Section 6).

**Step 3: Identify content that requires assets**

Mark posts that need:
- Canva carousel → schedule design time 2 days before
- Loom video → record 3 days before
- Case study → write 1 week before
- Product thumbnail → create before launch week

## 7. Weekly Content Routine

**Monday (30 min):**
- Review the month's calendar
- Write drafts for the week's 2–3 LinkedIn posts
- Draft the Thursday newsletter

**Tuesday (15 min):**
- Polish Monday drafts
- Create any carousel or visual assets needed for Wednesday

**Wednesday (10 min):**
- Publish Wednesday post
- Schedule Thursday email in Brevo

**Thursday (5 min):**
- Confirm email sends at 9am
- Reply to any comments from Mon/Wed posts

**Friday (15 min):**
- Publish Friday post
- Log week's metrics in Content Tracker (ZO-TRACK-004)
- Note: what performed best, what to repeat

---

## 8. Content Batching (Recommended Approach)

Batching = writing multiple posts in one session, then scheduling them.

**Weekly batch session:** Tuesday morning, 60–90 minutes.
- Write 3 LinkedIn posts
- Write newsletter
- Create 1 carousel if needed

Benefits: fewer context switches, better consistency, lower daily mental load.

Tools:
- **Buffer** or **LinkedIn native scheduling** for LinkedIn posts
- **Brevo** for email scheduling

## 9. Launch Content Plan (For Product Launches)

The week of a product launch, override the normal calendar:

| Day | Content |
|-----|---------|
| Launch Day -5 | "Something I've been working on…" (teaser) |
| Launch Day -3 | Behind-the-scenes: what's inside |
| Launch Day -1 | "Tomorrow it goes live" + early-bird if applicable |
| Launch Day | Launch post (LinkedIn + email to full list) |
| Launch Day +2 | Results so far / what buyers are saying |
| Launch Day +5 | "Last chance / price going up" |
| Launch Day +7 | Thank-you post + first buyer quote |

Every launch post links to the Gumroad listing (ZO-DIGI-002).

## 10. Evergreen Content Bank

Maintain a Google Doc: `Content_Bank.md` in the Marketing folder.

When a post performs well (100+ reactions, 5+ DMs, or becomes a reference): add it to the bank. Repurpose it 90 days later with a new angle.

Format:
```
---
Title: [Original post title]
Published: [Date]
Platform: [LinkedIn]
Performance: [X impressions, X reactions]
Repurpose idea: [e.g., turn into carousel / email / video script]
---
```

---

## 11. Content Repurposing Matrix

One idea, multiple formats:

| Original Format | Repurpose Into |
|----------------|----------------|
| LinkedIn text post (top performer) | Email newsletter body |
| Email newsletter | LinkedIn carousel (key points) |
| Client case study | LinkedIn story post + email feature |
| Tutorial Loom | LinkedIn "5 steps" text post |
| Carousel | Short video (record yourself talking through the slides) |

## 12. Topic Generation

Never run out of ideas. Use these sources:

**From work:**
- What did you build or fix for a client this week?
- What mistake did you make and how did you fix it?
- What question did a lead or client ask you?

**From audience:**
- What did people comment on your last post?
- What do people DM you about?
- What are people getting wrong about AI automation?

**From trends:**
- New tool launched in your space?
- Something major changed in n8n, Claude, or your stack?

Keep a running list in `Content_Ideas.md` — capture every idea immediately before it disappears.

## 17. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Planning only 1 week ahead** — too reactive; you'll miss launch windows and burn out scrambling for ideas
- **No pillar discipline** — without pillars, content becomes random and fails to build a coherent audience
- **Not logging metrics** — you can't improve what you don't measure; log every week even if it's just 3 numbers
- **Content for peers, not buyers** — founders posting for other founders get engagement but no clients; always ask "would my ICP care about this?"
- **Treating every post as a standalone** — the best content strategy is interconnected: posts reference products, products reference posts, emails drive back to LinkedIn
