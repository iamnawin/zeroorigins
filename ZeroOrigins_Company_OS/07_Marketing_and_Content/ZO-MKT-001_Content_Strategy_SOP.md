# ZO-MKT-001 — Content Strategy SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To define what ZeroOrigins publishes, why, and how content connects to revenue — not just engagement.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins content across LinkedIn, email, and other channels.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

At the start of each quarter; revisit monthly if something isn't working.

---

## 5. Content Mission Statement

> ZeroOrigins publishes content that helps founders and operators understand AI automation — so they become clients, buyers, or referral sources.

Every piece of content must connect back to one of these outcomes:
1. Attract leads (top-of-funnel awareness)
2. Build trust (demonstrate expertise)
3. Convert (drive to offer or product)

---

## 6. Content Pillars (What We Talk About)

| Pillar | % of Content | Examples |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| AI Automation Education | 40% | "How to automate X with n8n", tutorials |
| Behind-the-Scenes / Process | 25% | "How I built X", lessons learned |
| Client Results / Case Studies | 20% | Before/after, outcomes (anonymized if needed) |
| Offers / Products | 15% | Launch posts, product highlights |

Keep the mix: 85% value, 15% direct promotion.

---

## 7. Audience (Who We Write For)

Primary: **Solo founders and operators** in service businesses who know they need automation but don't know where to start.

Secondary: **Agency owners and consultants** who want to add automation to their service stack.

Write to the primary audience first. If it helps both, great.

---

## 8. Voice and Tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Write like you're explaining to a smart friend | Use jargon without explanation |
| Be direct and specific | Be vague ("AI can transform your business") |
| Share real examples and real numbers | Fake hype or inflated claims |
| Own your opinions | Hedge everything with "it depends" |
| Show the work, not just the result | Only post wins; acknowledge setbacks |

---

## 9. Content Formats by Platform

| Platform | Formats That Work | Frequency |
|----------|------------------|-----------|
| LinkedIn | Text posts, carousels, short videos | 3–5x/week |
| Email newsletter | Deep-dive how-tos, case studies | 1x/week |
| YouTube / Loom | Workflow walkthroughs, tutorials | 2x/month |

Don't spread thin across platforms. LinkedIn first, email second, everything else later.

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every post connects to a pillar
- [ ] Post is for the primary audience (solo founders / operators)
- [ ] Voice is direct and specific (passes the "vague-check")
- [ ] 15% or less of monthly posts are direct promotions

---

## 11. Approval Required

None — Founder writes and publishes directly.

---

## 12. Output

- Monthly content calendar (see ZO-MKT-002)
- Published posts logged in Content Tracker (ZO-TRACK-004)

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Marketing / Content_Strategy /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Posting without a pillar** — random content doesn't build an audience; it just fills a feed
- **Too much promotion** — audiences disengage when every post is "buy this"
- **Writing for peers** — other founders are not your buyer; write for the person one step behind you

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Quarterly strategy review; monthly pillar-mix check.
