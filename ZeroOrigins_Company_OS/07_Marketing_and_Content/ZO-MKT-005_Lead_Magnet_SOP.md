# ZO-MKT-005 — Lead Magnet SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To create and promote lead magnets that grow the ZeroOrigins email list with high-quality subscribers who match the ICP.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins free downloads, templates, and free resources used to capture leads.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When starting a new lead generation push, or when the email list growth stalls below 50 new subscribers/month.

---

## 5. What Makes a Good Lead Magnet

A lead magnet works when it:
- Solves **one specific problem** in under 10 minutes of reading/using
- Attracts the exact buyer you want (not everyone)
- Demonstrates your expertise naturally (it's a sample of your work)
- Has a logical next step (from free → paid)

**Test:** Would your ICP pay $5–$10 for this? If yes, it's good enough to be free.

---

| Type | Example | Build Time | Conversion |
|------|---------|------------|------------|
| Checklist | "10-Point Automation Audit" | 2 hours | Medium |

---

## 7. Step-by-Step Lead Magnet Launch Process

**Step 1: Build the asset**
- Use Google Docs → export PDF
- Or Notion → share as template
- Or n8n → export workflow JSON

**Step 2: Create the delivery page**
- Use a simple Tally form or Carrd landing page
- Fields: Name + Email only (never ask for more)
- Copy formula: Problem → Solution → What you get → [Download button]

**Step 3: Set up delivery**
- Brevo: create automation → trigger on form submit → send email with download link
- Subject: "Here's your [Lead Magnet Name]"

**Step 4: Promote it**
- LinkedIn post offering the lead magnet ("DM me 'MAGNET' to get it" or direct link)
- Add to LinkedIn bio link
- Add to email signature
- Mention in relevant conversations

---

## 8. LinkedIn Post Formula for Lead Magnets

```
I built a [format] that helps [who] [do specific thing].

[3 bullets on what's inside]

It's free.

[Simple CTA: "Drop a comment / DM me / Link in bio"]
```

This type of post consistently generates 100+ DMs when the ICP is right.

---

## 9. Lead Magnet Landing Page Copy Template

```
[Headline: Specific outcome in bold]

[Subheadline: who it's for + how fast they'll get the result]

What's inside:
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]

[Your name + 1-line credibility: "Built by Naveen, founder of ZeroOrigins — who has built 50+ automations for service businesses"]

[Download button / email form]

[Privacy note: "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."]
```

---

## 10. After They Download — Next Step

The thank-you / delivery email should include a soft next step:

```
P.S. — If you want me to build this for you (or something like it) for your business, 
reply "BUILD" and I'll share how we work together.
```

This converts a small % of free subscribers into discovery call bookings.

---

## 11. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Lead magnet solves one specific problem
- [ ] Only asks for Name + Email (no extra fields)
- [ ] Delivery email sends within 5 minutes of signup
- [ ] Thank-you email has a clear next step
- [ ] Promoted on LinkedIn within first week

Google Drive → `Marketing / Lead_Magnets / [MagnetName] /`

---

## 15. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Too broad a topic** — "AI for Business" attracts everyone and converts nobody; go specific
- **Too long** — a 40-page ebook is not a lead magnet; 5 pages max
- **Asking for too much info** — name + email only; every extra field kills conversion rate
- **No follow-up sequence** — a subscriber who hears nothing after downloading forgets you within a week

---

## 16. Review Frequency

Per lead magnet — check download-to-subscriber conversion rate monthly. Replace underperforming lead magnets quarterly.
