# ZO-MKT-007 — SEO SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To build organic search visibility for ZeroOrigins so the right people find us when they search for AI automation help.

---

## 2. Scope

ZeroOrigins website and any published content with a URL.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When building or updating the website; monthly for content SEO.

---

## 5. SEO Priority for ZeroOrigins (Phase 1)

At the current stage, SEO is a medium-term play — not the primary lead channel. LinkedIn and outreach are faster. But set up the basics now so the website starts ranking over time.

**Phase 1 goal:** Rank for 5–10 long-tail keywords that buyers actually search.

---

## 6. Target Keywords

Focus on **buyer-intent keywords** (people close to hiring or buying):

| Keyword | Intent | Monthly Volume (est.) |
|---------|--------|----------------------|
| n8n automation setup for small business | Buy | Low (high value) |
| hire AI automation consultant India | Buy | Low |
| lead follow-up automation | Research | Medium |
| automate client onboarding | Research | Medium |
| n8n workflow templates | Research | Medium |
| AI automation for consultants | Research | Low |

Use Google Search Console and Ubersuggest (free) to validate monthly volume.

---

## 7. On-Page SEO Checklist (Per Page)

| Element | What to Do |
|---------|-----------|
| Page title | Include primary keyword; under 60 characters |
| Meta description | 1–2 sentences with keyword; under 155 characters |
| H1 | One per page; includes primary keyword |
| H2s | Break content into sections; include secondary keywords |
| URL | Short and descriptive: `/services/lead-automation` not `/page?id=123` |
| Images | Alt text on every image describing what it shows |
| Internal links | Link from every page to at least 2 other pages on the site |
| Page speed | Under 3 seconds load time (check with PageSpeed Insights) |

---

## 8. Content SEO (Blog / Articles)

If writing articles for SEO:
- Minimum 800 words per article
- Target one primary keyword per article
- Answer the search intent completely — don't tease
- Include a real example or case study
- Add internal links to service pages and product pages

Article structure:
1. Hook / Why this matters (100 words)
2. The problem in detail (200 words)
3. The solution / How-to (400–600 words with steps)
4. Summary and next step (100 words)

---

## 9. Technical SEO Basics

- [ ] Site has SSL (https, not http)
- [ ] Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] Robots.txt is not blocking search engines
- [ ] No broken links (check with Broken Link Checker)
- [ ] Mobile-friendly (test with Google Mobile Friendly Test)
- [ ] Google Analytics or Plausible installed

---

## 10. Local SEO (India)

If targeting India-based clients:
- [ ] Google Business Profile created and verified
- [ ] NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistent across all listings
- [ ] Service description includes "AI automation consultant, [City], India"

---

## 11. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every service page has a unique title and meta description
- [ ] Google Search Console connected and showing impressions
- [ ] At least 1 new piece of content published per month (even LinkedIn articles count)
- [ ] Page speed under 3 seconds on mobile

---

## 12. Approval Required

None — Founder manages directly.

---

## 13. Output

- Optimized service pages
- Monthly content for SEO
- Google Search Console data reviewed monthly

---

## 14. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Marketing / SEO /`
- `Keyword_Tracker.xlsx`
- `SEO_Audit_[Date].md`

---

## 15. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Chasing high-volume keywords** — a solo founder cannot compete with established sites for "AI automation"; target low-volume, high-intent keywords
- **SEO instead of LinkedIn** — for ZeroOrigins at this stage, SEO is secondary; don't spend more than 2 hours/month on it in Year 1

---

## 16. Review Frequency

Monthly for Google Search Console metrics; quarterly for keyword review.
