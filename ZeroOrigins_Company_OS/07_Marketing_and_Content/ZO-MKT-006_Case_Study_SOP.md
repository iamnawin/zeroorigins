# ZO-MKT-006 — Case Study SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To document client results in a structured format that builds trust and converts prospects into clients.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins service delivery projects that produced a measurable result.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

After a project closes and the client has seen results — typically 2–4 weeks post-delivery.

---

## 5. When to Write a Case Study

Write a case study when:
- The client achieved a measurable outcome (time saved, leads captured, revenue recovered)
- The client gives permission (verbal or written confirmation)
- The work demonstrates a capability relevant to future buyers

You do NOT need to use the client's real name — anonymized case studies ("E-commerce founder in Bangalore") are valid and still effective.

---

## 6. Case Study Structure (500–800 words)

```markdown
# Case Study: [Outcome-First Title]
e.g., "How a solo recruiter recovered 15 hours/week with one automation"

## The Client
[1–2 sentences: who they are, what they do, how big]
"A solo recruiter in Mumbai managing 30+ active candidates per month."

## The Problem
[2–3 sentences: what was broken, what it was costing them]
"Every morning, she spent 2 hours manually updating a spreadsheet with candidate status, sending follow-up emails, and checking LinkedIn for replies. She was missing callbacks because she simply lost track."

## What We Built
[3–5 sentences: what ZeroOrigins delivered, what tools were used]
"We built an n8n automation that connected her Gmail, Airtable, and LinkedIn. When a candidate replies, the record updates automatically. At 8am daily, the system sends her a briefing with who to call and what to say."

## The Result
[Specific numbers: time saved, leads captured, revenue generated, errors eliminated]
- 2 hours/day saved on manual tracking
- 100% of follow-ups now sent on time
- 3 candidates placed in the first month after implementation (vs. 1 in the previous month)

## Client Quote
"[Direct quote, if permission given]"
— [Name / Role / City] OR "Mumbai-based recruiter"

## What Made This Work
[1–2 sentences on the key insight or approach]
"The key was connecting the tools she already used — no new platforms, no training required. The automation fit her existing workflow."

---
*Built by ZeroOrigins — AI Automation for Service Businesses*
*Want this for your business? [Book a call / visit link]*
```

---

## 7. Getting Client Permission

Email template to request permission:

```
Subject: Can I share what we built together?

Hi [Name],

Now that [project] is live, I'd love to write a short case study on what we built and the results you've seen.

I'd keep it anonymized (no company name or full name without your approval).

Would that be okay with you? If yes, I'll send you a draft before I publish anything.

— Naveen
```

---

## 8. Case Study Formats

| Format | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| Written (500–800 words) | Google Drive, email, LinkedIn article |
| LinkedIn post version (300 words) | Adapted from full case study for feed post |
| Slide deck (5 slides) | For proposals and pitches |
| Video walkthrough (Loom, 5–8 min) | For website or email |

Start with written. Repurpose from there.

---

## 9. Where to Use Case Studies

- Proposal documents (ZO-SALES-003) — most important
- LinkedIn posts (ZO-MKT-003) — drives inbound discovery calls
- Email newsletter (ZO-MKT-004) — nurtures existing list
- Product pages — shows proof that the system works
- Discovery calls — mention as reference during the call

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Client has given permission (documented)
- [ ] Outcome is specific (number, time, money, error rate — not "they were happy")
- [ ] Problem → Solution → Result structure followed
- [ ] Case study saved to Drive AND added to Proposal Bank (ZO-SALES-003)
- [ ] LinkedIn version published within 2 weeks of writing

---

## 11. Approval Required

Client permission required. Founder writes the case study.

---

## 12. Output

- Written case study (Google Doc)
- LinkedIn post version
- Added to Proposal Bank

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Marketing / Case_Studies / [ClientNickname_or_Industry] /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Publishing without permission** — even anonymized, always confirm with the client first
- **Vague results** — "the client was very happy" is not a case study; find the number
- **Waiting too long** — write it while the details are fresh (within 2 weeks of project close)

---

## 15. Review Frequency

After every closed project. Target: 1 new case study per month.
