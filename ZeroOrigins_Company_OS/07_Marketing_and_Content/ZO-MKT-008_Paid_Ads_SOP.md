# ZO-MKT-008 — Paid Ads SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To define when, where, and how ZeroOrigins runs paid advertising — and when NOT to run it.

---

## 2. Scope

Any paid advertising spend by ZeroOrigins across LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, or Google Ads.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Only after organic channels (LinkedIn, email) are generating consistent leads AND you have at least one proven offer with 3+ sales.

---

## 5. When Not to Run Ads

Do NOT run paid ads until:
- [ ] You have 3+ client case studies (social proof to retarget with)
- [ ] You know your ICP precisely (who buys, why, at what price)
- [ ] Organic content generates at least 2 inbound DMs/week
- [ ] Budget is at least ₹15,000/month (below this, data is insufficient to optimize)

Paid ads amplify what already works — they don't fix a broken offer or unclear positioning.

---

## 6. Channel Guide

| Platform | Best For | Minimum Budget |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B service leads (founders, operators) | ₹20,000/month |
| Meta Ads | Digital product sales (lower CPM) | ₹10,000/month |
| Google Ads | Capture existing demand (search intent) | ₹15,000/month |

Start with one platform. Do not split budget across all three.

---

## 7. LinkedIn Ads (Recommended First Channel)

LinkedIn Ads work for ZeroOrigins because the ICP (founders, operators, solo consultants) spends time there.

**Campaign type:** Lead Gen Form (collects name + email without leaving LinkedIn)

**Targeting:**
- Job titles: Founder, CEO, Operations Manager, Business Owner, Director
- Company size: 1–50 employees
- Industry: Consulting, IT Services, Professional Services
- Location: India → expand to USA/UAE after India works

**Ad format:** Single Image + short copy (see template below)

**LinkedIn Ad Copy Template:**
```
Headline: [Outcome in 5–7 words]
e.g., "Automate Your Lead Follow-Up in 2 Days"

Body (150 chars max):
"[Pain point] → [Solution] → [Proof].
[CTA — e.g., Download the free template]"

e.g., "Manual follow-ups killing your close rate?
We've automated this for 20+ service businesses. Get the free setup guide."

CTA button: Download / Learn More / Get Quote
```

---

## 8. Meta Ads (For Digital Products)

Use Meta for product launches and lead magnets — lower CPM than LinkedIn.

**Campaign objective:** Traffic (to Gumroad) or Lead Generation

**Audience:**
- Custom audience: Email list upload (min 500 contacts)
- Lookalike: 1% lookalike of buyer list
- Interest targeting: Entrepreneurship, Automation, No-code tools

**Ad format:** Single image or short video (15–30 seconds)

---

## 9. Budget and Tracking

**Before launching:**
- [ ] UTM parameters on all ad links: `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=[campaign-name]`
- [ ] Google Analytics / Plausible tracking confirmed on landing page
- [ ] Conversion goal set (form submit, purchase, or call booking)

**Budget allocation:**
- Start: ₹500/day for 2 weeks (testing phase)
- If CPA is acceptable: Scale to ₹1,000–₹2,000/day
- If CPA is too high after 2 weeks: Pause and revise creative or targeting

**Acceptable CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):**
| Goal | Max CPA |
|------|---------|
| Email subscriber | ₹200 |
| Discovery call booked | ₹1,500 |
| Digital product sale ($29 product) | ₹500 |

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Organic channels are working before ads are launched
- [ ] Case studies exist to use as social proof in ads
- [ ] UTM parameters on all ad links
- [ ] Conversion tracking confirmed
- [ ] Budget set with a hard stop date for the test phase

---

## 11. Approval Required

Founder decision; do not run ads without an explicit budget decision in writing (even a note to yourself).

---

## 12. Output

- Active ad campaign with tracking
- Weekly performance report logged in Marketing tracker

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Marketing / Paid_Ads / [Platform] / [CampaignName] /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Running ads before organic proof** — you'll waste money learning what free content would have taught you
- **No UTM tracking** — you won't know which ad drove the sale
- **No hard stop budget** — ad platforms spend eagerly; set daily and total campaign budget limits
- **Too many platforms at once** — master one before adding another

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Weekly during active campaigns; monthly when paused.
