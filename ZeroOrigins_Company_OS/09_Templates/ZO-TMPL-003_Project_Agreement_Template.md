## 5. Intellectual Property

- The Service Provider retains the right to reference the project for portfolio, case study, and marketing purposes, subject to Client approval on the specific content used.

## 6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to:
- Keep all project-related information confidential
- Not disclose the other party's proprietary information to third parties without written consent
- This obligation continues for 2 years after project completion

## 7. Limitation of Liability

The Service Provider's maximum liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees paid for the specific project. The Service Provider is not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages arising from the use of delivered systems.

---

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 7 days' written notice. If terminated by the Client after work has begun:
- Work completed to date is billed at the agreed rate
- The advance is non-refundable if termination occurs after build has started

If terminated by the Service Provider:
- A pro-rated refund of the advance for uncompleted work will be returned
