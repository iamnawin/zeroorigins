## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including but not limited to:

- Business strategies, plans, and pricing
- Client names, contact details, and project details
- n8n workflow designs, automation logic, and AI system architectures
- AI prompts, embeddings, and model configurations
- Source code, APIs, and technical documentation
- Financial information, costs, and revenue data
- Any other information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the context

---

## 2. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
3. Use Confidential Information only for the purpose of the agreed engagement
4. Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
5. Notify the Disclosing Party promptly upon discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure

---

## 3. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

- Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Was already known to the Receiving Party before disclosure
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt prior notice to the Disclosing Party)

---

## 4. Work Product and IP Assignment

Any work product, code, designs, documents, workflows, or other deliverables created by the Receiving Party in connection with the engagement are the sole property of ZeroOrigins. The Receiving Party hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product to ZeroOrigins.

---

## 5. Term

This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for **2 years** after the end of the engagement. Obligations regarding trade secrets continue indefinitely.

---

## 6. Return of Information

Upon request or at the end of the engagement, the Receiving Party will:
- Return all Confidential Information and copies
- Delete digital copies from personal devices and cloud storage
- Confirm in writing that this has been done
