# ZO-FIN-007 — Refund Policy SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To handle refund requests fairly and consistently, protecting client trust while maintaining business stability.

---

## 2. Scope

All refund requests: custom projects, digital products, workshops.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When a client or buyer requests a refund.

---

## 5. Refund Policy by Product/Service Type

### Custom Automation Projects

| Stage | Refund |
|-------|--------|
| Before work starts | 100% refund of advance |
| After discovery call, before build | 80% refund of advance |
| After architecture/design delivered | 50% refund of advance |
| After build started (>30% done) | No refund — credit toward future work |
| After delivery | No refund — only fix/revision |

### Digital Products (Gumroad)

- Refund window: 7 days from purchase
- Condition: Product must not have been downloaded and resold
- Refund: 100% if requested within 7 days, no questions asked (Gumroad policy aligns)
- After 7 days: No refund, but we will help resolve issues

### Workshops

- Refund window: 48 hours before the workshop start
- Cancellation by ZeroOrigins: 100% refund automatically
- No-show without notice: No refund
- After workshop: No refund for digital access already provided

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Client submits refund request**
- Acknowledge within 24 hours: "Thank you for reaching out. I'll review your request and respond within 2 business days."

**Step 2: Investigate**
- Check what was delivered vs. what was promised
- Check which policy stage applies
- Check if there's a genuine issue (not delivered, defective) vs. buyer's remorse

**Step 3: Decision**
- Apply policy table above
- If genuinely at fault (wrong delivery, missed deadline): offer full refund regardless of policy
- If client is dissatisfied but work was delivered: offer revision before refund

**Step 4: Process refund**
- For Razorpay/Stripe: process via platform dashboard
- For bank transfer: send to original payment account with note "Refund — ZO-INV-XXXX"
- For Gumroad: process through Gumroad dashboard

**Step 5: Update records**
- Mark invoice as "Refunded" in Invoice Tracker
- Note reason in tracker
- File refund confirmation in Google Drive

**Step 6: Analyze and prevent**
- Every refund = a learning opportunity
- Note: what went wrong? Was it a client expectation issue? Delivery issue?
- Update process if recurring refund pattern emerges

Founder approval for all refunds.

---

## 9. Output

---

## 10. Storage Location

Invoice Tracker + Google Drive → `Finance / Invoices /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **No clear refund policy stated** — leads to disputes. State the policy in every proposal and product listing.
- **Delaying refund response** — creates negative reviews and word-of-mouth damage
- **Processing refund without GST adjustment** — if a GST invoice was raised, a credit note must be issued to reverse the GST. Consult CA.

---

## 12. Review Frequency

As needed. Review policy annually.
