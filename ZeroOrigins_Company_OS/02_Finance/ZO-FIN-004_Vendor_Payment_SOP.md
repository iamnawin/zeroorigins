# ZO-FIN-004 — Vendor Payment SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To process payments to vendors, contractors, and service providers correctly, including TDS deduction where applicable.

---

## 2. Scope

All outgoing payments: freelancers, subcontractors, tool vendors, professional services.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When a vendor/contractor invoice is received and approved.

---

## 5. TDS Rules (Consult CA to confirm current thresholds)

| Payment Type | TDS Section | Rate | Threshold |
|-------------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Contractor / freelancer (individual) | 194C | 1% | Rs. 30,000 single payment or Rs. 1,00,000 in a year |
| Professional services (CA, lawyer, etc.) | 194J | 10% | Rs. 30,000 in a year |
| Rent (office space) | 194I | 10% | Rs. 2,40,000 in a year |
| International payments | 195 | As applicable | Consult CA |

**Important:** If you deduct TDS, you must:
- Deposit with government by 7th of following month
- File TDS return quarterly
- Issue TDS certificate (Form 16A) to the deductee

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Receive vendor invoice**
- Verify: vendor name, bank details, GST invoice (if they're registered), amount, description

**Step 2: Check TDS applicability**
- Is this vendor an individual or company?
- Does the payment exceed the threshold?
- Check with CA if uncertain

**Step 3: Calculate payment amount**
- If TDS applicable: Payment to vendor = Invoice amount - TDS
- Example: Invoice Rs. 50,000, TDS @1% = Rs. 500. Pay Rs. 49,500 to vendor.

**Step 4: Process payment**
- Transfer via NEFT/IMPS to vendor's bank account
- Note UTR number

**Step 5: Log in expense tracker**
- Date, vendor, amount, TDS deducted, payment method, UTR

**Step 6: Deposit TDS with government**
- By 7th of following month via TDS challan (ITNS 281 on tax.gov.in)
- CA can do this for you

**Step 7: Issue TDS certificate**
- Issue Form 16A to vendor within 15 days of TDS return filing date
- CA generates this

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Vendor invoice reviewed and approved
- [ ] TDS applicability checked
- [ ] Payment made and UTR noted
- [ ] Expense logged
- [ ] TDS deposited by 7th of month
- [ ] Form 16A issued to vendor quarterly

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder approval for all vendor payments above Rs. 10,000.

---

## 9. Output

- Payment confirmation (UTR)
- TDS deposited and recorded
- Expense logged

---

## 10. Storage Location

Expense Tracker + receipts in Google Drive → `Finance / Expenses /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not deducting TDS when required** — disallows the expense as a deduction and attracts penalty
- **Missing TDS deposit deadline** — attracts interest (1.5% per month)
- **Not collecting vendor GST invoice** — you lose ITC

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Monthly. SOP review annually.
