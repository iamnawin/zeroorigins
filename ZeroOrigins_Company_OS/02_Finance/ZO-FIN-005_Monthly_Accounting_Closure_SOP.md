# ZO-FIN-005 — Monthly Accounting Closure SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To close the books cleanly every month so ZeroOrigins always knows its financial position, tax liability, and runway.

---

## 2. Scope

End-of-month accounting tasks for revenue, expenses, GST, and bank reconciliation.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder (with CA executing accounting entries)

---

## 4. Trigger

First week of each month (for previous month closure).

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Collect all documents by 5th of month**
- [ ] All sales invoices issued (from Invoice Tracker)
- [ ] All expense receipts (from expense folder)
- [ ] Bank statement for the month (export from bank portal)
- [ ] Any credit card or debit card statements

**Step 2: Reconcile bank statement**
- Match each bank entry to an invoice or expense record
- Flag unmatched transactions for investigation
- Confirm all invoiced amounts were received as expected

**Step 3: Review accounts receivable (AR)**
- Identify all invoices still unpaid at month end
- Age them: 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 60+ days
- Initiate follow-ups (see ZO-FIN-002)

**Step 4: Check GST liability**
- Calculate total GST collected on sales invoices
- Calculate ITC earned from vendor invoices
- Net GST payable = GST collected - ITC

**Step 5: Share data with CA**
- Email/share Google Drive folder with all documents
- Subject: "ZeroOrigins — [Month Year] — Accounting Data"

**Step 6: CA posts entries and shares P&L**
- CA posts all transactions in Tally / QuickBooks / Zoho Books
- CA sends monthly P&L statement and Balance Sheet snapshot

**Step 7: Review financials**
- Check: Revenue vs target, Expenses vs budget, Net profit/loss
- Identify any unusual items

**Step 8: Pay GST by 20th**
- Based on GSTR-3B prepared by CA
- Pay via net banking to GST portal

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] All invoices and receipts collected by 5th
- [ ] Bank reconciliation complete
- [ ] AR aged and follow-ups initiated
- [ ] GSTR-1 filed by 11th
- [ ] GSTR-3B filed and tax paid by 20th
- [ ] P&L reviewed by founder
- [ ] Accounting software entries confirmed by CA

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder reviews and approves monthly P&L before CA closes the month.

---

## 9. Output

- Monthly P&L statement
- Balance Sheet snapshot
- Filed GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
- Reconciled bank statement

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Finance / Monthly Reports / FY2026-27 /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not closing monthly** — backlog creates confusion at year end and stresses the CA
- **Not reconciling bank** — creates "mystery money" that's hard to explain later
- **Delaying GST payment** — attracts interest from due date

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Monthly process. SOP review quarterly.
