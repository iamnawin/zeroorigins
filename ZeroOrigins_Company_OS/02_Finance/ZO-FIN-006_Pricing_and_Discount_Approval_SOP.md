# ZO-FIN-006 — Pricing and Discount Approval SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure ZeroOrigins pricing is consistent, margins are protected, and discounts are only given when they serve a strategic purpose.

---

## 2. Scope

All services, retainers, digital products, workshops, and custom projects.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When setting price for a new service, product, or client; when a client requests a discount.

---

## 5. ZeroOrigins Pricing Framework

### Service Pricing (Current Base Rates)

| Service | Base Price | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| LeadFlow Setup (basic) | Rs. 15,000 / $200 | Lead capture + follow-up automation |
| LeadFlow Setup (advanced) | Rs. 35,000 / $450 | With AI scoring and CRM integration |
| Full Automation System | Rs. 75,000–1,50,000 / $1,000–$2,000 | Custom n8n system |
| Monthly Retainer (maintenance) | Rs. 10,000–25,000 / $150–$350 | Monitoring, updates, support |
| Workshop (online) | Rs. 5,000–15,000 / $75–$200 per participant | |
| Digital Product (Gumroad kit) | Rs. 999–4,999 / $15–$75 | Templates, starter kits |

### Pricing Rules

1. **Never go below Rs. 10,000 / $150 for any custom automation project** — below this, margins don't justify the support overhead.
2. **International clients: always price in USD, AUD, or AED** — never Indian rupee equivalent for offshore billing.
3. **Retainers must cover at least 4 hours of work per month** — estimate time before quoting.
4. **Digital products: once priced, don't discount without a reason** — protect price integrity.

## 6. Discount Policy

### When Discounts Are Acceptable

| Scenario | Maximum Discount | Condition |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Upfront full payment (100% advance) | 10% | Only for projects >Rs. 50,000 |
| Long-term retainer (6+ months prepaid) | 15% | Must pay full amount upfront |
| Referral from existing client | 10% | Applied to referral client's first invoice |
| Launch/pilot pricing (new service) | Up to 30% | Time-limited; max 3 clients |
| Genuine financial hardship (vetted) | Case by case | Founder decision only |

### When to Never Discount

- To win a deal where the client is haggling without reason
- On already-low-priced digital products
- As a standard response to any pushback (hold your price first)
- For "exposure" or "portfolio building" once you have 2+ case studies

### How to Handle Discount Requests

1. **First response:** "Our pricing reflects the time, tools, and expertise required for this outcome. Let me check if there's a way to scope it down to fit your budget."
2. **Second option:** Offer a smaller scope at lower price — not a discount on the same scope.
3. **If genuinely offering a discount:** Frame it as a benefit: "Since you're paying upfront, I can apply a 10% discount."

## 7. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: New service/product pricing**
- Estimate total time (hours × hourly rate)
- Add tool costs, overhead, support buffer (30%)
- Apply minimum margin target: 50% gross margin minimum
- Compare with market rates (check competitors)
- Set price and document in Pricing Register

**Step 2: Client requests discount**
- Do not respond immediately — say "Let me check what's possible"
- Review: is this a strategic client? First project? Referral?
- If discount is warranted, choose the appropriate type from policy above
- Document the discount given in Invoice Tracker (notes column)

**Step 3: Update pricing register**
- If you change a service price, update the Pricing Register
- Note: old price, new price, reason, date

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Discounting without tracking** — creates pricing inconsistency and unhappy clients who compare notes
- **Underpricing custom work** — AI automation projects require ongoing support; cheap upfront = loss overall
- **Matching competition blindly** — ZeroOrigins delivers outcomes, not hours. Price on value.
