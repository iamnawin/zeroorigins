# ZO-FIN-002 — Payment Collection SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure ZeroOrigins collects payments on time, follows up systematically on overdue invoices, and maintains healthy cash flow.

---

## 2. Scope

All client payments: project fees, retainers, digital product sales, workshop fees.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

After any invoice is raised.

---

## 5. Payment Methods Supported

| Method | Best For | Fee |
|--------|----------|-----|
| Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS) | Indian clients, large amounts | Free |
| UPI | Indian clients, quick payments | Free |
| Razorpay Payment Link | Indian clients, professional experience | 2% |
| Stripe | International clients | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| PayPal | International clients (small amounts) | 3.5%+ |
| Wise | International clients (large amounts, lower fee) | 0.5–1% |

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

### Standard Collection Flow

**Step 1: Send invoice with payment instructions**
- Include UPI ID, bank details, and Razorpay/Stripe link in every invoice
- State payment terms clearly: "Payment due within [X] days of invoice date"
- Recommended terms: Net 7 for project milestones; Net 30 for retainers

**Step 2: Confirm invoice received**
- Send WhatsApp or email 24 hours after sending invoice: "Hi [Name], sharing invoice ZO-INV-2026-005 for [service]. Let me know if anything needs clarification. Payment details are in the invoice."

**Step 3: Day 1 overdue — First follow-up**
- Friendly: "Hi [Name], the invoice for [service] was due on [date]. Just checking if payment was processed."
- Check Invoice Tracker — update with follow-up date

**Step 4: Day 7 overdue — Second follow-up**
- More direct: "Hi [Name], following up on invoice ZO-INV-2026-005 (Rs. X). Could you confirm expected payment date? Happy to help if there are any issues with the invoice."

**Step 5: Day 14 overdue — Third follow-up + escalation**
- Phone call preferred over message
- Re-send invoice PDF
- If MSME client in India: remind them politely that under MSMED Act, MSMEs are entitled to compound interest on overdue amounts beyond 45 days

**Step 6: Day 21+ — Final notice**
- Send formal email: "This is a final reminder for invoice [#], due [date], amount [X]. Please confirm payment by [date+7]. If payment is not received, we may need to pause all ongoing work."
- Do not threaten legal action unless you are genuinely prepared to follow through

**Step 7: Payment received**
- Confirm receipt via email/WhatsApp: "Payment received. Thank you!"
- Update Invoice Tracker: Status → Paid, Payment Date → [date]
- Record in accounting software

### Advance Payment Policy

For new clients or high-risk engagements, require advance payment:
- New client: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
- Large project (>Rs. 50,000): 30% upfront, 40% at mid-milestone, 30% on completion
- International client: 100% upfront or 50/50 split
- Digital products: 100% upfront (via Gumroad/Stripe)

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Payment terms stated on invoice
- [ ] All payment methods listed on invoice
- [ ] Invoice Tracker reviewed weekly
- [ ] Follow-ups sent on schedule
- [ ] Advance payment collected from new clients
- [ ] Payments reconciled in accounting software monthly

---

## 8. Approval Required

None for standard follow-up. Founder approval before pausing work or threatening legal action.

---

## 9. Output

- Paid invoice
- Updated Invoice Tracker
- Bank reconciliation entry

---

## 10. Storage Location

Invoice Tracker: Google Sheets (live)

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Doing work without payment terms agreed** — always agree on price and terms before starting
- **Not following up** — clients don't pay late out of malice; they're busy. Follow-up systematically.
- **Accepting payment for cash-heavy Indian projects without invoice** — creates GST and income tax exposure
- **Not keeping payment confirmations** — save all UTR numbers and payment receipts

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Weekly (review Invoice Tracker). SOP review quarterly.
