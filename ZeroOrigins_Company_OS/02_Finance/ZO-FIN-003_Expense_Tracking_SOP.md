# ZO-FIN-003 — Expense Tracking SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To record every business expense accurately so that tax deductions are maximized, GST input credit is claimed, and the CA has clean data for accounting.

---

## 2. Scope

All company expenses: tools, subscriptions, freelancer payments, travel, office, marketing, and professional services.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Every time money leaves the company bank account or is spent on a business credit/debit card.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

- Expense receipts or invoices (PDF or photo)
- GST invoice from vendor (for ITC claim)
- Payment confirmation (UPI receipt, bank SMS, credit card statement)

---

## 6. Common ZeroOrigins Expense Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Software & Tools | n8n Cloud, OpenAI API, Notion, Figma, AWS, Google Workspace |
| Freelancer / Contractor | Developer, designer, content writer payments |
| Marketing | Paid ads, LinkedIn Premium, content creation costs |
| Professional Services | CA fees, legal fees, trademark fees |
| Office | Internet, co-working space, stationery |
| Travel | Client meetings, conferences |
| Domain / Hosting | Domain renewal, server hosting |
| Education | Courses, books, subscriptions |

---

## 7. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Make the purchase**
- Always use company bank account or company credit card
- Never mix personal and company expenses

**Step 2: Save the receipt immediately**
- Download invoice/receipt as PDF
- Save to Google Drive: `Finance / Expenses / FY2026-27 / [Month] / [Category] /`
- If GST invoice: ensure it has your company GSTIN

**Step 3: Log in expense tracker**
- Open Expense Tracker (Google Sheets or accounting software)
- Add row: Date, Vendor, Category, Amount, GST (if any), Payment Method, Receipt saved (Y/N)

**Step 4: Monthly reconciliation**
- At month end, match expense tracker with bank statement
- Every transaction in bank should have a matching expense entry
- Flag any unexplained transactions

**Step 5: Share with CA**
- Export expense tracker and folder of receipts to CA by 5th of following month
- CA will post entries in accounting software and reconcile ITC

---

## 8. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every expense has a receipt saved
- [ ] All vendor invoices with GSTIN collected for ITC
- [ ] Expense logged within 48 hours of transaction
- [ ] Monthly bank reconciliation complete
- [ ] No personal expenses mixed in

---

## 9. Approval Required

None below Rs. 10,000. Founder self-approves. Expenses above Rs. 50,000: document rationale in notes.

---

## 10. Output

- Monthly expense report
- Receipts folder organized by month
- Reconciled bank statement

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Finance / Expenses / FY2026-27 /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not saving receipts** — you cannot claim tax deduction without proof
- **Personal spending on company account** — creates accounting confusion and tax issues
- **Not collecting GST invoices** — you lose ITC claims worth up to 18% of each expense
- **Forgetting annual software subscriptions** — budget for renewals in advance

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Monthly. SOP review annually.
