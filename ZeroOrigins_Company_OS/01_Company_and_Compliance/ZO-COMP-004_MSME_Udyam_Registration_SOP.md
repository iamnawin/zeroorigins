# ZO-COMP-004 — MSME Udyam Registration SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To register ZeroOrigins under MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) via the Udyam portal to access government benefits, priority lending, and payment protection under MSME laws.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to initial Udyam registration and annual update.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Register as soon as the company is incorporated. This is free and takes 10–15 minutes. No CA needed.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

- Company PAN
- Aadhaar of authorized signatory (founder)
- GSTIN (if registered)
- Bank account details
- NIC / business activity code

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1:** Go to [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Click "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME"

**Step 3:** Enter Aadhaar number and name → Validate via OTP

**Step 4:** Select enterprise type:
- Select "Company" if incorporated as Pvt Ltd
- Otherwise select as applicable

**Step 5:** Enter business details:
- Name of enterprise
- PAN
- Location / address
- Date of commencement
- Bank account number and IFSC
- NIC code — for AI/software services, use **62099** (Other IT services)
- Investment in plant and machinery/equipment
- Annual turnover

**MSME Classification (services sector):**
| Category | Investment | Turnover |
|----------|------------|----------|
| Micro | Up to Rs. 1 Cr | Up to Rs. 5 Cr |
| Small | Up to Rs. 10 Cr | Up to Rs. 50 Cr |
| Medium | Up to Rs. 50 Cr | Up to Rs. 250 Cr |

ZeroOrigins at early stage: **Micro Enterprise**

**Step 6:** Submit and receive Udyam Registration Number (URN) and Certificate

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Aadhaar verified via OTP
- [ ] Correct NIC code entered
- [ ] Udyam certificate downloaded and stored
- [ ] URN noted in Compliance Tracker

---

## 8. Approval Required

None — self-filing.

---

## 9. Output

- Udyam Registration Certificate with URN
- MSME classification (Micro / Small / Medium)

---

## 10. Storage Location

- Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Admin / Compliance /`
- Physical copy filed in incorporation folder

---

## 11. Benefits of MSME Registration

- Priority sector lending from banks
- Lower interest rates on some loans
- Protection under MSMED Act: buyers must pay within 45 days or pay interest
- Access to government tenders reserved for MSMEs
- Some state government subsidies and schemes

---

## 12. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not updating annually** — Udyam requires annual self-declaration; update by 31 March each year
- **Incorrect turnover figures** — use actual figures; don't inflate to move up a category (you lose micro benefits)

---

## 13. Review Frequency

Annually (update by 31 March each year on Udyam portal).
