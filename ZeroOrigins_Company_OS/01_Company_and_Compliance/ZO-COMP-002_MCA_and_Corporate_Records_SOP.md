# ZO-COMP-002 — MCA and Corporate Records SOP

> **Consult CA/CS professional for all statutory filings. This SOP provides operational guidance only.**

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure all MCA filings are completed on time and all statutory registers and corporate records are maintained correctly.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all annual and event-based MCA filings for ZeroOrigins Private Limited.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder (with CA/CS executing filings)

---

## 4. Trigger

- Annual: triggered by financial year end (31 March each year)
- Event-based: triggered when directors change, address changes, capital changes, etc.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

- Certificate of Incorporation
- Company PAN
- Financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow)
- Board resolutions
- Minutes of Annual General Meeting (AGM)
- Director KYC documents
- DSC of directors

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

### Annual Filings

**Step 1: Board Meeting — Financial statement approval**
- Hold board meeting before AGM
- Approve audited financial statements
- Pass resolution to call AGM

**Step 2: Annual General Meeting (AGM)**
- Must be held within 6 months of financial year end
- For April–March FY, deadline is 30 September
- Agenda: adopt financial statements, appoint/re-appoint auditor, declare dividend (if any)
- Prepare and circulate AGM notice 21 days in advance

**Step 3: File MGT-7 (Annual Return)**
- Due: within 60 days of AGM
- Contains: list of shareholders, directors, charges, etc.
- Filed by CA/CS on MCA portal
- Penalty for late filing: Rs. 100/day

**Step 4: File AOC-4 (Financial Statements)**
- Due: within 30 days of AGM
- Attach: Balance Sheet, P&L, Director's Report, Auditor's Report
- Filed by CA/CS on MCA portal

**Step 5: File DIR-3 KYC (Director KYC)**
- Due: 30 September each year
- All directors must file annually to keep DIN active
- Your CA/CS can assist

### Event-Based Filings

| Event | Form | Deadline |
|-------|------|----------|
| Director appointment | DIR-12 | 30 days |
| Director resignation | DIR-12 + DIR-11 | 30 days |
| Change of registered address | INC-22 / INC-22A | 30 days |
| Change of company name | INC-1, INC-24 | Varies |
| Increase in authorised capital | SH-7 | 30 days |
| Allotment of new shares | PAS-3 | 30 days |
| Creation of charge (loan/mortgage) | CHG-1 | 30 days |

### Statutory Registers (Maintain Permanently)

The Companies Act, 2013 requires companies to maintain these registers:

- Register of Members (MGT-1) — list of shareholders
- Register of Directors (MBP-1) — director details
- Register of Contracts (MBP-4) — related party transactions
- Minutes Book — all board meeting and AGM minutes
- Register of Share Allotments

These can be maintained digitally but must be produced on demand by authorities.

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Missing AGM deadline** — attracts penalty for directors personally
- **Not filing DIR-3 KYC** — DIN gets deactivated, blocking future filings
- **Not maintaining minutes book** — required by Companies Act, can create problems during audits or due diligence
- **Director resigning without filing DIR-11** — creates liability exposure
