# ZO-COMP-006 — Annual Compliance Calendar SOP

> **Consult CA/CS to confirm current deadlines. Government deadlines change. This is a reference guide only.**

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure ZeroOrigins never misses a statutory or regulatory deadline, avoiding penalties, late fees, and legal complications.

---

## 2. Scope

All annual, quarterly, and monthly compliance requirements for a Private Limited Company in India.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder (CA/CS executes most filings)

---

## 4. Trigger

Review at start of each financial year (April 1) and update as regulations change.

---

## 5. Annual Compliance Calendar

### Monthly (Every Month)

| Date | Task | Filed By | Form |
|------|------|----------|------|
| 7th | TDS deposit (if you deduct TDS on contractor/vendor payments) | Founder / CA | — |
| 11th | GSTR-1 filing (sales invoices) | CA | GSTR-1 |
| 20th | GSTR-3B filing + GST payment | CA | GSTR-3B |

### Quarterly

| Quarter | Task | Deadline | Form |
|---------|------|----------|------|
| Q1 (Apr–Jun) | TDS return (if applicable) | 31 July | 26Q / 27Q |
| Q2 (Jul–Sep) | TDS return | 31 October | 26Q / 27Q |
| Q3 (Oct–Dec) | TDS return | 31 January | 26Q / 27Q |
| Q4 (Jan–Mar) | TDS return | 31 May | 26Q / 27Q |
| Advance Tax | Advance tax installment (if liable) | 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar | — |

### Annual

| Deadline | Task | Filed By | Form |
|----------|------|----------|------|
| 30 April | Auditor appointment (Board Resolution) | CA | ADT-1 |
| 30 June | Due date for payment of advance tax (last installment) | CA | — |
| 31 July | Income Tax Return (ITR) for individuals (founders) | CA | ITR-1/2/3 |
| 30 September | DIR-3 KYC for all directors | CA | DIR-3 KYC |
| 30 September | AGM deadline (6 months from FY end) | Founder | — |
| 30 October | AOC-4 filing (Financial Statements) | CA | AOC-4 |
| 29 November | MGT-7 / MGT-7A filing (Annual Return) | CA | MGT-7 |
| 15 October | Income Tax Return for companies (audit mandatory) | CA | ITR-6 |
| 31 December | GSTR-9 annual GST return | CA | GSTR-9 |

### Event-Based (File Within 30 Days of Event)

| Event | Form | Deadline |
|-------|------|----------|
| Director appointment/resignation | DIR-12 | 30 days |
| Registered office change | INC-22 | 30 days |
| Share allotment | PAS-3 | 30 days |
| Loan taken (charge creation) | CHG-1 | 30 days |

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1:** At start of April each year, review this calendar with CA

**Step 2:** Set calendar reminders 7 days before each deadline

**Step 3:** Ensure data is ready for CA at least 5 days before deadline:
- Monthly: share invoice data by 5th of each month
- Annual: complete accounts by 15 April; give CA all statements by 30 April

**Step 4:** Confirm each filing via email from CA with acknowledgment number

**Step 5:** Log filed status in Compliance Tracker

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Annual compliance calendar reviewed with CA in April
- [ ] Monthly GST reminders set
- [ ] TDS deducted on all applicable payments (contractors > Rs. 30,000/year)
- [ ] Advance tax estimated and paid quarterly
- [ ] DIR-3 KYC filed by 30 September
- [ ] AGM held by 30 September
- [ ] AOC-4 filed by 30 October
- [ ] MGT-7 filed by 29 November
- [ ] GSTR-9 filed by 31 December
- [ ] Company ITR filed by 15 October

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder must sign all forms requiring director signature before CA submits.

---

## 9. Output

All filings on record with acknowledgment numbers in Compliance Tracker.

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Admin / Compliance / [Year] /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Missing DIR-3 KYC** — DIN deactivates, all future MCA filings blocked
- **Late AGM** — attracts penalty on directors personally
- **Forgetting TDS** — if you pay contractors above threshold without deducting TDS, you cannot claim it as expense, and you face penalty
- **Not maintaining advance tax estimates** — large tax due at year end + interest

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Annually (April) + whenever government releases new circulars.
