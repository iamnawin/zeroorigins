# ZO-COMP-007 — Document Retention SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To define how long ZeroOrigins retains different types of business documents and where they are stored, so nothing important is deleted prematurely and nothing is hoarded unnecessarily.

---

## 2. Scope

All company documents: financial, legal, operational, client, and compliance records.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Review annually in April. Also triggered when a document is created (decide retention on creation, not later).

---

## 5. Retention Schedule

| Document Type | Minimum Retention | Notes |
|--------------|-------------------|-------|
| Incorporation documents (CoI, MoA, AoA) | Permanent | Never delete |
| Company PAN, TAN | Permanent | Never delete |
| Board resolutions and minutes | Permanent | Required by Companies Act |
| Statutory registers | Permanent | Required by Companies Act |
| Tax returns (ITR, GST) | 8 years | IT Act requirement |
| GST invoices (issued and received) | 8 years | GST Act requirement |
| Financial statements | 8 years | Companies Act |
| Bank statements | 8 years | |
| Contracts and agreements | 6 years after end | |
| Client project files | 5 years after project close | |
| Employee/contractor records | 8 years after separation | |
| IP documents (patents, trademarks) | Permanent | |
| Email correspondence (client) | 3 years | |
| Marketing materials | 2 years | |
| Draft documents (unapproved) | 1 year | |

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1:** When creating any document, label it with:
- Document type
- Date created
- Retention period
- Storage location

**Step 2:** Organize Google Drive with clear year-based folders

**Step 3:** Set a calendar reminder each April to review and archive/delete expired documents

**Step 4:** Before deleting, confirm nothing is under legal hold (active dispute, audit, investigation)

**Step 5:** Archive expired documents to `12_Archive/` for 1 additional year before final deletion

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] All incorporation documents stored permanently in Google Drive and physical file
- [ ] Financial records tagged with 8-year retention
- [ ] Client files archived after project closure
- [ ] Annual review done in April
- [ ] No documents deleted that are under active retention period

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder approval before any permanent deletion.

---

## 9. Output

Clean, organized document repository with clear retention labels.

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Admin / [Category] / [Year] /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Deleting GST invoices before 8 years** — required for GST audit
- **Not backing up physical documents digitally** — fire, flood, or loss creates permanent gaps
- **Keeping everything forever** — wastes storage and creates confusion in search

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Annually (April).
