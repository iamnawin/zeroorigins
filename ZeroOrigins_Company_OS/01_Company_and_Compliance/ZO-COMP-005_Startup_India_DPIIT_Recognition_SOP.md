# ZO-COMP-005 — Startup India DPIIT Recognition SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To obtain DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) recognition under the Startup India scheme for access to tax exemptions, fast-track IP protection, and government scheme access.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to the initial DPIIT recognition application. The company must qualify as a startup by DPIIT criteria.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

After company incorporation and MSME registration. Apply at any point within the first 10 years of incorporation if turnover has not exceeded Rs. 100 Cr.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

- Certificate of Incorporation
- Company PAN
- Details of founders and directors
- Brief description of business and innovation
- Any proof of uniqueness (product concept, tech used, use of AI, etc.)
- Funding received (if any)
- Turnover details

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1:** Go to [startupindia.gov.in](https://startupindia.gov.in)

**Step 2:** Create account as "Startup" → Complete profile

**Step 3:** Fill "DPIIT Recognition" application:
- Company details
- Nature of business (Innovation / Scalability / New Product/Service)
- Describe how ZeroOrigins is innovative:
  > "ZeroOrigins builds AI-native automation systems and agentic workflows that apply large language models and n8n automation to business process problems. The company develops proprietary AI products and digital automation templates with a scalable delivery model."
- Upload CoI, PAN, and brief deck if required

**Step 4:** Submit application

**Step 5:** DPIIT reviews — typically 2–4 weeks

**Step 6:** Receive DPIIT Recognition Number and Certificate

---

## 7. Key Benefits After Recognition

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Tax Holiday | 3-year income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC (requires DPIIT + CBDT approval — consult CA) |
| Angel Tax Exemption | Section 56(2)(viib) exemption on investments received |
| Fast-track IP | 80% rebate on patent fees; expedited patent examination |
| Self-certification | Labour and environmental laws — self-certify compliance for 3–5 years |
| Government tenders | Exempted from prior experience and turnover criteria |
| Fund of Funds | Access to SIDBI-managed Fund of Funds |

---

## 8. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Application completed on startupindia.gov.in
- [ ] Innovation narrative clearly describes AI/automation angle
- [ ] DPIIT certificate received and downloaded
- [ ] Recognition number stored in Compliance Tracker

---

## 9. Approval Required

None — self-filing. CA may assist with tax exemption applications after recognition.

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Admin / Compliance /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Weak innovation narrative** — DPIIT looks for genuine innovation; generic descriptions may lead to rejection. Emphasize AI, agentic workflows, and novel product approach.
- **Not tracking expiry of tax exemptions** — consult CA to actually claim the 80-IAC exemption; it's separate from just having DPIIT recognition.

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Once at registration. Update profile annually on startupindia.gov.in.
