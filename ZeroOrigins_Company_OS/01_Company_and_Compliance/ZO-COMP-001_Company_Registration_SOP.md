# ZO-COMP-001 — Company Registration SOP

> **Consult a CA/CS professional before initiating any registration process. This SOP provides operational guidance only, not legal advice.**

---

## 1. Purpose

To guide the founder through the complete process of registering ZeroOrigins as a legal business entity in India — from name selection to receiving the Certificate of Incorporation.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to: Initial company formation only. Covers Private Limited Company registration via MCA SPICe+ process. Also references LLP and Proprietorship as alternatives.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder (with CA/CS support)

---

## 4. Trigger

Run this SOP once when ZeroOrigins is ready to become a legal entity. This should be the first formal business action taken.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

**Founder Documents (each director):**
- [ ] PAN card (self-attested copy)
- [ ] Aadhaar card (self-attested copy)
- [ ] Passport-size photograph (white background)
- [ ] Address proof: utility bill (not older than 2 months), bank statement, or Aadhaar
- [ ] Email address and mobile number linked to Aadhaar

**Registered Office Proof:**
- [ ] Rent agreement (if rented) — notarized preferred
- [ ] No-objection certificate (NOC) from owner if using residential address
- [ ] Utility bill for the address (not older than 2 months)

**Company Details:**
- [ ] Proposed company name (2–3 options in order of preference)
- [ ] Business objects / activities (what the company will do)
- [ ] Authorised share capital amount (typically Rs. 1,00,000 for early stage)
- [ ] Paid-up capital amount
- [ ] Director DIN numbers (obtained during SPICe+ filing)

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

### Phase 1: Pre-Incorporation Decisions

**Step 1: Choose your entity type**

| Entity | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Private Limited Company | Investor-ready, limited liability, credible | Higher compliance (annual ROC filings, audits) | If you plan to raise funding or work with large clients |
| Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) | Lower compliance than Pvt Ltd, limited liability | Cannot issue equity shares easily | Professional services, 2-person partnerships |
| One Person Company (OPC) | Solo founder, limited liability | Cannot have more than 1 director | Solo founder, small operations |
| Proprietorship | Fastest, cheapest | No liability protection, less credible | Very early stage only |

**Recommendation for ZeroOrigins:** Private Limited Company. Enables future funding, instils client confidence, and allows equity distribution to co-founders or employees.

**Step 2: Finalize company name**
- Rules for Pvt Ltd names:
  - Must end with "Private Limited" or "Pvt. Ltd."
  - Cannot be identical or too similar to existing registered companies
  - Cannot be a registered trademark of another entity
  - Should reflect your business activity
- Options to consider:
  - ZeroOrigins Technologies Private Limited
  - ZeroOrigins AI Systems Private Limited
  - ZeroOrigins Labs Private Limited

**Step 3: Check name availability**
- Go to: [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in) → MCA Services → Company/LLP Name Search
- Also check: [ipindia.gov.in](https://ipindia.gov.in) → Trade Marks → Public Search
- Also check: domain availability (namecheap.com or GoDaddy) for your chosen name

**Step 4: Draft MoA objects clause**
The Objects Clause defines what your company is legally allowed to do. Be broad enough to cover all 5 business lines.

Sample main objects for ZeroOrigins:
```
1. To carry on the business of designing, developing, and delivering artificial 
   intelligence systems, machine learning models, large language model applications, 
   agentic workflows, and automation software for businesses.

2. To develop, license, and sell software products, SaaS platforms, digital templates, 
   automation tools, and AI-powered applications.

3. To provide training, workshops, online courses, and educational content on 
   artificial intelligence, automation, and related technology topics.

4. To produce, license, and distribute AI-generated creative content including music, 
   video, visual art, and digital media.

5. To provide technology consulting, systems integration, API development, and 
   workflow automation services to businesses and organizations.
```

---

### Phase 2: DSC and DIN

**Step 5: Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**
- Required for all proposed directors
- Apply through any government-approved certifying authority (e.g., eMudhra, Sify, NSDL)
- Documents: PAN, Aadhaar, photo, and video-based verification
- Turnaround: 1–3 business days
- Cost: Rs. 1,000–2,500 per DSC
- DSC type needed: Class 3 (individual)

**Step 6: Director Identification Number (DIN)**
- DIN is automatically allotted during SPICe+ form submission
- No separate application needed for new companies

---

### Phase 3: SPICe+ Filing

**Step 7: Engage CA/CS**
- Share all documents listed in Section 5
- Ask them to prepare:
  - SPICe+ Form (Part A: name reservation + Part B: incorporation)
  - eMoA (INC-33) — electronic Memorandum of Association
  - eAoA (INC-34) — electronic Articles of Association
  - AGILE-PRO-S form (PAN, TAN, GSTIN, bank account, ESIC/EPFO)

**Step 8: Review draft MoA and AoA**
- Ensure all 5 business lines are covered in the Objects Clause
- Review director details and shareholding structure
- Sign digitally using DSC

**Step 9: Submit SPICe+ on MCA portal**
- Your CA/CS will submit the form
- Government fee: Rs. 0 (for companies with authorised capital up to Rs. 15L, effective from 2018)
- State stamp duty: varies by state

**Step 10: Track application status**
- Check on mca.gov.in → Track Payment Status
- Typical processing time: 5–10 working days
- You will receive via email:
  - Certificate of Incorporation (CoI)
  - Company Identification Number (CIN)
  - PAN of company
  - TAN of company

---

### Phase 4: Post-Incorporation

**Step 11: Receive and verify Certificate of Incorporation**
- Download from MCA portal
- Verify: company name, CIN, date of incorporation, registered address
- Print and store 2 certified copies

**Step 12: Apply for company bank account**
- Open a current account in the company's name
- Required documents:
  - Certificate of Incorporation
  - MoA and AoA
  - Board Resolution for bank account (CA drafts this)
  - PAN of company
  - Address proof of registered office
  - KYC of all directors
- Recommended banks: HDFC, ICICI, Kotak (better startup support and online banking)

**Step 13: Invest paid-up capital**
- Transfer subscribed capital amount from personal account to company account
- This satisfies the paid-up capital requirement

**Step 14: Apply for GST**
- See SOP `ZO-COMP-003_GST_Registration_and_Filing_SOP.md`

**Step 15: Register for MSME (Udyam)**
- See SOP `ZO-COMP-004_MSME_Udyam_Registration_SOP.md`

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using a name that conflicts with existing trademark** — always check IPIndia before filing
- **Narrow MoA objects** — if your objects don't cover a business activity, you technically cannot do it legally. Be broad but specific.
- **Wrong registered address** — must be a valid, reachable address. Courier from RoC/MCA will arrive here.
- **Skipping MSME and DPIIT** — these are free, fast, and give benefits. Don't skip them.
- **Not opening company bank account quickly** — any payments before the account is open complicate accounting.
- **Mixing personal and company money** — never use personal account for company transactions.
