# ZO-PROD-006 — Launch Checklist SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To run a systematic pre-launch check so nothing critical is missed when releasing a new product.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins product launches: SaaS, digital products, templates, courses.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

3–5 days before a planned product launch.

---

## 5. Pre-Launch Checklist (Run 5 Days Before)

### Product

- [ ] QA completed and signed off (ZO-PROD-005)
- [ ] All core features work end-to-end
- [ ] Error states handled
- [ ] Mobile-friendly (if web product)
- [ ] Download/access works correctly (for digital products)

### Pricing and Payments

- [ ] Price confirmed and finalized
- [ ] Payment link live and tested (Gumroad / Razorpay / Stripe)
- [ ] Test purchase completed successfully
- [ ] Receipt/confirmation email sent on test purchase
- [ ] Refund policy documented and linked

### Content and Listing

- [ ] Product name and tagline finalized
- [ ] Description written (problem → solution → who it's for → what's included → price)
- [ ] Screenshots or demo video ready
- [ ] Thumbnail / cover image created (1200x630px minimum)
- [ ] FAQ section written

### Legal / Compliance

- [ ] Terms of service linked (even if simple)
- [ ] Privacy policy linked
- [ ] Refund policy stated
- [ ] If collecting personal data: DPDPA compliance considered (consult legal if needed)

### Marketing / Launch Plan

- [ ] Launch date and time confirmed
- [ ] LinkedIn post drafted and scheduled
- [ ] Email to existing list drafted (if applicable)
- [ ] ProductHunt / AppSumo listing prepared (if applicable)
- [ ] DMs/outreach list prepared (10–20 warm contacts to notify)

### Analytics

- [ ] Analytics tracking added to landing page (Google Analytics or Plausible)
- [ ] Conversion tracking set up (did purchase happen after visit?)
- [ ] UTM links created for each traffic source

---

## 6. Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Post goes live at planned time
- [ ] Payment link is live
- [ ] Email sent to list
- [ ] DMs sent to warm contacts
- [ ] Monitor for purchases, sign-ups, or DMs
- [ ] Respond to all comments and questions same day

---

## 7. Post-Launch (Day 3–7)

- [ ] Count: sales, clicks, conversions
- [ ] Review: what content drove traffic?
- [ ] Follow up with anyone who clicked but didn't buy (if you have data)
- [ ] Collect feedback from early buyers

---

## 8. Quality Checklist

- [ ] All items in the 5-day pre-launch checklist done
- [ ] Test purchase completed
- [ ] Launch post scheduled

---

## 9. Approval Required

Founder sign-off before going live.

---

## 10. Output

- Live product listing
- First sales within 7 days (goal)

---

## 11. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / [ProductName] / Launch /`

---

## 12. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Launching without testing purchase flow** — nothing kills a launch like a broken buy button
- **No launch content prepared** — launching without telling anyone is not a launch
- **No analytics** — you won't know what worked

---

## 13. Review Frequency

Before every product launch.
