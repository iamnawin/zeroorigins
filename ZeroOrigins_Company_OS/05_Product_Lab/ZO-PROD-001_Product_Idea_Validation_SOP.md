# ZO-PROD-001 — Product Idea Validation SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To validate product ideas before spending time building them — so ZeroOrigins only builds products that real people want and will pay for.

---

## 2. Scope

All new product ideas: SaaS, tools, templates, courses, automation kits.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When a new product idea is identified and added to the Product Idea Tracker.

---

## 5. Validation Framework

Use the 3-question test before starting any product:

1. **Is there a real problem?** Can you describe it in one sentence without using the word "better"?
2. **Will people pay for a solution?** Is there existing paid competition? Are people spending money on workarounds?
3. **Can ZeroOrigins build it in a reasonable time?** (Under 2 weeks for an MVP)

If the answer to all 3 is yes: validate further. If any answer is unclear: research first.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Document the idea**
Add to Product Idea Tracker with:
- Product name (working title)
- The problem it solves
- Who has this problem (target user)
- How they solve it today
- Estimated price people would pay
- How long to build v1

**Step 2: Market validation (30 minutes)**
- Google the problem: are people searching for it?
- Check Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn for people discussing this problem
- Check ProductHunt, Gumroad, AppSumo for similar products
- Note: how many competitors, what do they charge?

**Step 3: Demand signal check**
Pick one of these:
- Post on LinkedIn: "I'm building [X]. Who would find this useful?" — if 10+ responses: validated
- DM 5 target users: "Quick question — do you deal with [problem]? How do you handle it?" — if 3/5 say they struggle: validated
- Create a basic landing page with email signup — if 20 signups in a week: validated

**Step 4: Willingness to pay**
Ask directly: "Would you pay Rs. 2,000 / $25 for a tool that [outcome]?"
Or: "If I build this, would you be an early buyer at [price]?"

Pre-sales are the best validation.

**Step 5: Go / No-Go Decision**
| Signal | Score |
|--------|-------|
| 10+ people have this problem | 2 |
| Existing competitors charging money | 2 |
| 3+ people said they'd pay | 3 |
| 1+ pre-sale confirmed | 5 |
| Under 2 weeks to build | 1 |

Score 8+: Build it. Score 5–7: Validate more. Score <5: Shelve it.

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Problem clearly defined in one sentence
- [ ] Market research completed (30 min minimum)
- [ ] At least 5 real people contacted about the problem
- [ ] Willingness to pay tested
- [ ] Go/No-Go decision documented in Product Idea Tracker

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder decision.

---

## 9. Output

- Product Idea Tracker updated with validation status
- Go/No-Go decision recorded

---

## 10. Storage Location

Product Idea Tracker: `10_Trackers/Product_Idea_Tracker.md`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Building before validating** — the #1 startup mistake
- **Asking "would you use it?" instead of "would you pay for it?"** — people say yes to use, not to pay
- **Counting followers as validation** — engagement ≠ paying customers

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Before every product build.
