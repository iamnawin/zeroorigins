# ZO-PROD-007 — User Feedback SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To systematically collect, categorize, and act on user feedback after product launch.

---

## 2. Scope

All ZeroOrigins digital products, SaaS, and templates post-launch.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Within 7 days of launch, then ongoing monthly.

---

## 5. Feedback Collection Methods

| Method | When | Tool |
|--------|------|------|
| Post-purchase survey | Immediately after purchase | Tally / Google Form |
| 7-day follow-up email | 7 days after purchase | Brevo / Mailchimp |
| 1:1 call (early adopters) | First 10 buyers only | Calendly booking |
| Social monitoring | Ongoing | Manual check |
| App in-product | If web product | Tally embed |

---

## 6. Post-Purchase Survey (3 Questions Max)

Keep it short — long surveys kill response rates.

```
1. What made you decide to buy this? (open text)
2. Have you used it yet? If yes, what's working? If no, what stopped you? (open text)
3. What's the one thing that would make this 10x better? (open text)
```

Send within 24 hours of purchase. Target: 30%+ response rate.

---

## 7. 7-Day Follow-Up Email Template

```
Subject: Quick question about [Product Name]

Hi [Name],

It's been a week since you picked up [Product Name].

One quick question: did you get the result you were hoping for?

If yes — what worked?
If no — what got in the way?

Either way, hit reply. I read every response.

— Naveen
```

---

## 8. Feedback Categorization

When you receive feedback, tag it:

| Tag | What It Means |
|-----|--------------|
| `UX` | Confusing interface or flow |
| `MISSING` | Feature or content they expected but isn't there |
| `BUG` | Something broken |
| `PRICING` | Price too high, value unclear |
| `ONBOARDING` | Hard to get started |
| `PRAISE` | Positive — save for testimonials |
| `IDEA` | Enhancement idea for future versions |

---

## 9. Monthly Feedback Review Process

On the last Friday of each month:
1. Pull all feedback from survey tool and email replies
2. Tag each item using the table above
3. Count: how many per tag?
4. Identify the top 2 issues by volume
5. Decide: fix now (v1.1) or queue for v2?

---

## 10. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Post-purchase survey live and linked
- [ ] 7-day follow-up email scheduled in email tool
- [ ] First 10 buyers offered 1:1 call
- [ ] Feedback log exists in Google Sheets

---

## 11. Approval Required

None — Founder manages directly.

---

## 12. Output

- Tagged feedback log (Google Sheets)
- Monthly feedback summary (1 page)
- Shortlist of top 3 improvements for next version

---

## 13. Storage Location

Google Drive → `Products / [ProductName] / Feedback /`

---

## 14. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Ignoring feedback** — early feedback is gold; act on it fast or buyers churn
- **Too many survey questions** — 3 questions max; more = zero responses

---

## 15. Review Frequency

Monthly, ongoing.
