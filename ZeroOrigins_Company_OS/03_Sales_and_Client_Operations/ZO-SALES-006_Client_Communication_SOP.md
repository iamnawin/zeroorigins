# ZO-SALES-006 — Client Communication SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To maintain professional, clear, and timely communication with all clients so they feel informed and confident throughout the project.

---

## 2. Scope

All client communications during active projects and post-delivery.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Communication Channels

| Channel | Use Case |
|---------|---------|
| Email | Formal communication, proposals, invoices, agreements |
| WhatsApp | Quick updates, scheduling, informal check-ins |
| Zoom / Google Meet | Discovery calls, kickoff, demo, reviews |
| Loom video | Async walkthroughs and demos |
| Shared Google Drive folder | Document exchange |

---

## 5. Communication Standards

**Response time:**
- Email: respond within 24 hours on business days
- WhatsApp: respond within 4 hours during business hours (9am–7pm IST)
- If you need more time: acknowledge and set expectations ("Looking into this, will revert by tomorrow")

**Tone:**
- Professional but warm
- Confident, not apologetic
- Clear and direct — avoid vague language

**Weekly project update (for active projects >1 week long):**
Send every Friday:
> "Hi [Name], quick update for this week:
> - Done: [what was completed]
> - In progress: [what's currently being built]
> - Blockers: [anything you need from them]
> - Next week: [what's planned]
>
> You're on track for delivery on [date]."

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Before a meeting:**
- Send agenda 24h before
- Confirm timing 1h before

**During a project:**
- Send weekly updates every Friday
- Notify immediately if there's a delay or blocker

**At delivery:**
- Send handover email with all files, credentials, and documentation
- Schedule walkthrough session

**After delivery:**
- Follow up 7 days post-delivery to check everything is working
- Request testimonial (see ZO-SALES-007)

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Response time under 24h for emails, 4h for WhatsApp
- [ ] Weekly updates sent every Friday for active projects
- [ ] Delays communicated proactively (not after the fact)
- [ ] Meeting agendas sent in advance
- [ ] Handover email sent with all deliverables

---

## 8. Approval Required

None for standard communication.

---

## 9. Output

- Clear, documented communication thread per client

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Clients / [ClientName] / Communication /` (for important emails)

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Going silent during build** — clients assume something is wrong if they don't hear from you
- **Over-promising timelines** — pad your timelines and deliver early rather than under-delivering
- **Sending bad news via WhatsApp** — important issues (delay, scope change, pricing update) go via email

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Quarterly.
