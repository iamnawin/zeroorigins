# ZO-SALES-001 — Lead Capture SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To capture every incoming lead consistently so that no potential client falls through the cracks and every lead is followed up.

---

## 2. Scope

All inbound and outbound leads: LinkedIn, website form, referrals, Gumroad buyers, Instagram DMs, email, and direct messages.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Any time someone expresses interest in ZeroOrigins services or products.

---

## 5. Lead Sources and Capture Points

| Source | Capture Tool | Next Action |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Website contact form | Google Form / Tally.so → Sheets | Auto-log to Lead Tracker |
| LinkedIn DM | Manual | Log within 24 hours |
| Referral (word of mouth) | WhatsApp / Call | Log immediately |
| Cold outreach response | Email | Log immediately |
| Gumroad buyer | Gumroad dashboard | Log weekly |
| Instagram DM | Manual | Log within 24 hours |
| Workshop inquiry | Email / form | Log immediately |

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Lead comes in**
- Do not let a lead sit unlogged for more than 24 hours

**Step 2: Qualify quickly (60-second check)**
Ask yourself:
- Do they have a budget? (Can they afford Rs. 15,000+?)
- Do they have a problem we solve?
- Are they a decision-maker?
If yes to 2/3: move to active. If no: still log, but deprioritize.

**Step 3: Log in Lead Tracker**
Minimum fields to capture:
- Name
- Company
- Source
- Contact info (email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp)
- What they need (brief note)
- Date first contact
- Status: New

**Step 4: Respond within 24 hours**
- Message: "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! I'd love to understand more about what you're looking to build. Can I book a quick 30-minute call? Here's my Calendly: [link]"
- For cold outreach responses: personalize response based on their reply

**Step 5: Book discovery call**
- Send Calendly link
- Log in Lead Tracker: Status → Call Scheduled

**Step 6: Follow up if no response to booking link**
- Day 2: follow up once
- Day 5: one final follow up
- Day 7: mark as "Nurture" if no response — they go into a monthly check-in cycle

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every lead logged within 24 hours
- [ ] Response sent within 24 hours
- [ ] Lead Tracker updated after every interaction
- [ ] No lead at "New" status for more than 48 hours

---

## 8. Approval Required

None.

---

## 9. Output

- Lead logged in Lead Tracker
- Discovery call scheduled or lead in nurture

---

## 10. Storage Location

Lead Tracker: Google Sheets (live, always open)

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Not logging leads from WhatsApp/DM** — these feel informal but are real leads
- **Delayed response** — prospects move on fast, especially if they messaged 5 people
- **Logging but not acting** — a lead tracker that doesn't drive actions is useless

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Daily (check for new leads). SOP review monthly.
