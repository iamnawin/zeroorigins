# ZO-SALES-003 — Proposal Creation SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To create compelling, clear proposals that convert discovery calls into paid projects. A good proposal reflects the client's problem back to them, proposes a specific solution, and makes it easy to say yes.

---

## 2. Scope

All custom project proposals for ZeroOrigins automation services and AI systems.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

Within 48 hours of completing a discovery call where there is a clear fit.

---

## 5. Inputs Required

- Discovery call notes
- Client's business description and key pain point
- Budget indication from discovery call
- Relevant past work / case study if available
- Proposal template (`09_Templates/Proposal_Template.md`)

---

## 6. Proposal Structure

A ZeroOrigins proposal has 7 sections. Keep total length to 2–3 pages for standard projects.

---

### Section 1: Understanding Your Situation

*Summarize their problem in their own words. This shows you listened.*

**Template:**
> "[Company Name] is currently facing [specific problem]. The manual process of [what they do today] is consuming [time/effort] and leading to [consequence — missed leads, delayed responses, lost revenue]. You're looking for a solution that [desired outcome]."

Do not write generic intro. Copy their exact words from call notes.

---

### Section 2: What We Propose

*A clear, specific description of what you will build.*

**Template:**
> "We will design and build a [name of the system] using n8n and [tools relevant to their stack]. This system will:
> - [Outcome 1 — e.g., automatically capture leads from your website form]
> - [Outcome 2 — e.g., score and route leads to the right team member]
> - [Outcome 3 — e.g., send personalized follow-up messages via WhatsApp]
> - [Outcome 4 — e.g., log all activity to your Google Sheets CRM]"

Be specific. Avoid "we will improve your workflow." Say exactly what nodes/steps will be automated.

---

### Section 3: What You Get (Deliverables)

List all tangible outputs:

- [ ] n8n workflow (exported JSON file, deployed and live)
- [ ] Configuration documentation (how it works, what to change)
- [ ] 1x live walkthrough session (30 minutes)
- [ ] 30-day support window (bug fixes only)
- [ ] Credentials guide (what API keys are needed and how to set them up)

Optionally:
- [ ] Google Sheet template (if applicable)
- [ ] Email/WhatsApp templates (if messaging automation)

---

### Section 4: Timeline

| Milestone | Deliverable | Timeline |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Kickoff call | Finalize requirements | Day 1 |
| Architecture review | Workflow logic diagram | Day 3 |
| First build | Working beta | Day 7 |
| Client review | Feedback session | Day 8–9 |
| Revisions | Final adjustments | Day 10–11 |
| Delivery | Live deployment + handover | Day 12–14 |

Adjust timeline based on project complexity. Always pad by 20%.

---

### Section 5: Investment

State pricing clearly and with confidence.

**Simple format:**

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| LeadFlow Automation System | Rs. 25,000 |
| 30-day post-delivery support | Included |
| Monthly maintenance (optional) | Rs. 8,000/month |
| **Total Project Investment** | **Rs. 25,000** |

**Payment Terms:**
- 50% upfront (Rs. 12,500) to confirm project
- 50% on delivery (Rs. 12,500) upon live deployment

**Validity:** This proposal is valid for 7 days.

---

### Section 6: Why ZeroOrigins

2–3 sentences. Not a company bio — a reason to choose you.

> "We've built automation systems specifically for [their industry / use case]. We don't just set up tools — we document everything so your team stays in control. And we stay involved until it works."

Add one relevant case study or outcome if available:
> "For a [type of client], we built a similar lead-to-CRM system that reduced manual data entry by 4 hours/week and increased response speed from 24 hours to under 2 hours."

---

### Section 7: Next Steps

Make it a one-click action:

> "To move forward, reply to this email with 'Let's go' or confirm the advance payment of Rs. 12,500 to [payment details]. I'll send a kickoff form within 24 hours and we can start on [start date]."

---

## 7. Proposal Delivery

**Format:** PDF or shared Google Doc link (not Word)

**Subject line:** "Proposal: [System Name] for [Company Name] — [Date]"

**Email body:**
"Hi [Name], here's the proposal we discussed. It covers what we'll build, what you'll get, and what it costs.

[Link or attachment]

I'm happy to jump on a quick call to walk through it if helpful. Let me know if you have questions.

Valid for 7 days. Looking forward to working together."

---

## 8. Follow-Up After Proposal

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 (send day) | WhatsApp: "Sent proposal over email — let me know when you've had a chance to review." |
| Day 3 | Email: "Checking in on the proposal — any questions?" |
| Day 6 | Final follow-up before expiry: "Proposal expires tomorrow — happy to extend if you need more time." |
| Day 10 | Mark as "Stale" in Lead Tracker if no response |

---

## 9. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Problem statement reflects their exact words from call
- [ ] Deliverables are specific, not vague
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (include buffer)
- [ ] Pricing is clearly stated
- [ ] Payment terms stated
- [ ] Next step is a single, clear action
- [ ] Proposal sent within 48 hours of call
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled in calendar

---

## 10. Approval Required

None for standard proposals. For proposals above Rs. 1,00,000 or $1,500: review once more before sending.

---

## 11. Output

- Sent proposal (PDF)
- Lead Tracker updated: Status → Proposal Sent
- Follow-up scheduled

---

## 12. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Sales / Proposals / [ClientName_Date] /`

---

## 13. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Generic proposal** — if you could send it to anyone, the client can tell. Always reflect their specific situation.
- **Too long** — 2–3 pages maximum. Long proposals lose readers.
- **Vague deliverables** — "we'll improve your workflow" is not a deliverable. Be specific.
- **No expiry date** — add "valid for 7 days." Creates urgency.
- **No follow-up** — most proposals close on follow-up, not on first read. Follow up consistently.
- **Sending without reviewing** — read the proposal out loud once before sending. Fix anything that sounds odd.

---

## 14. Review Frequency

Review win/loss pattern quarterly. Update proposal structure based on what's working.
