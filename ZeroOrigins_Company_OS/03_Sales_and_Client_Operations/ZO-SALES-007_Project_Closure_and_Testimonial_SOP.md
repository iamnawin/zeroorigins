# ZO-SALES-007 — Project Closure and Testimonial SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To close projects cleanly, collect final payment, document outcomes, and turn happy clients into case studies and referrals.

---

## 2. Scope

All custom projects at delivery.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When the final deliverable is accepted by the client.

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Final delivery email**
- Subject: "Delivery — [Project Name] — ZeroOrigins"
- Include: all files, live link (if applicable), credentials summary, documentation
- State: "Support window of 30 days starts today for any bugs in the delivered workflow."

**Step 2: Walkthrough session**
- 30-minute session to walk client through the system
- Record with permission (Loom or Zoom recording)
- Give client time to test and ask questions

**Step 3: Collect final payment**
- Send final invoice on delivery day
- Do not close the project in your tracker until final payment is received

**Step 4: Internal handover notes**
- Write 5-bullet summary: what was built, key decisions, known limitations, anything the client should monitor
- Store in Google Drive client folder

**Step 5: Request testimonial (7 days after delivery)**
Send via WhatsApp or email:

> "Hi [Name], it's been great working with you on [project]. If you found it valuable, would you be willing to share a quick testimonial? Even 2–3 sentences about the problem you had and what changed would be amazing.
>
> You can reply here or use this form: [Google Form link]
>
> No pressure at all — but it really helps us grow."

Testimonial form should ask:
- Your name and company
- What problem you were trying to solve
- What ZeroOrigins built for you
- What result you've seen
- Would you recommend ZeroOrigins?

**Step 6: Request referral (optional, same message or follow-up)**
> "Also — if you know anyone who'd benefit from similar automation, I'd love an intro. Happy to offer 10% off your next project for any referral who becomes a client."

**Step 7: Close project in tracker**
- Mark project as Closed in Client Project Tracker
- Note final revenue, project type, outcome
- File all documents to client archive folder

**Step 8: Create case study outline (within 30 days)**
See ZO-MKT-007 for case study SOP. Use testimonial + project data to create one.

---

## 6. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Final delivery email sent with all files
- [ ] Walkthrough session completed
- [ ] Final invoice raised and paid
- [ ] Internal handover notes written
- [ ] Testimonial requested at Day 7 post-delivery
- [ ] Project closed in tracker
- [ ] Case study planned

---

## 7. Approval Required

None.

---

## 8. Output

- Closed project record
- Testimonial received (ideally)
- Case study in progress

---

## 9. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Clients / [ClientName] / Project_Archive /`

---

## 10. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Chasing testimonial too early** — wait at least 7 days; let them experience the value first
- **Not documenting internal notes** — if the same client comes back or something breaks, you need to remember what was built
- **Not closing the project formally** — projects that drag on create ambiguity about scope and payment

---

## 11. Review Frequency

After each project. SOP review quarterly.
