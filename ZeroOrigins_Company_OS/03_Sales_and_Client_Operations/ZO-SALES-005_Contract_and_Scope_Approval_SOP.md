# ZO-SALES-005 — Contract and Scope Approval SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To protect ZeroOrigins and the client by having a written agreement for all projects, defining scope, payment terms, ownership, and what happens when things change.

---

## 2. Scope

All projects above Rs. 25,000 / $300. Optional but recommended for smaller projects too.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

After proposal is accepted and before work begins.

---

## 5. Contract Elements (Minimum)

For ZeroOrigins, a simple "Letter of Engagement" or "Project Agreement" is sufficient for early stage. You do not need an elaborate legal contract for every project. Use the following as minimum content:

**1. Parties**
- ZeroOrigins Technologies Private Limited (CIN: XXXXXXXXX)
- [Client legal name and address]

**2. Project Description**
- What will be built (reference the proposal)
- Technology stack (n8n version, integrations)

**3. Deliverables**
- List exactly what will be delivered (reference Section 3 of proposal)

**4. Timeline**
- Start date
- Expected delivery date
- What happens if client delays providing inputs: timeline shifts accordingly

**5. Payment Terms**
- Total fee
- Payment schedule (advance %, delivery %)
- Payment method
- Late payment terms (e.g., work pauses if payment is delayed 7+ days)

**6. Scope Change Policy**
> "Any requirements added after the kickoff call are considered out of scope and will be quoted separately. Changes to core requirements after build starts will extend the timeline and may attract additional charges."

**7. Intellectual Property**
> "Upon receipt of full payment, the client owns the workflow configuration files delivered. ZeroOrigins retains ownership of any underlying proprietary logic, prompt templates, or AI systems used to build the solution."

**8. Confidentiality**
> "Both parties agree to keep the other's business information confidential."

**9. Limitation of Liability**
> "ZeroOrigins is not liable for third-party service interruptions (n8n, OpenAI, Google, etc.) or for data loss due to the client's infrastructure."

**10. Governing Law**
> "This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes will be resolved in [your city] jurisdiction."

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1:** After proposal acceptance, prepare the project agreement

**Step 2:** Send via email with request to reply "I agree" or sign
- For projects above Rs. 1,00,000: use a simple PDF signature (DocuSign free tier or simply printing + scanning)
- For standard projects: email reply confirmation is acceptable

**Step 3:** Store signed agreement in Google Drive

**Step 4:** Reference the agreement number in the invoice

---

## 7. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Agreement covers all 10 elements above
- [ ] Scope clearly defined (reference proposal)
- [ ] Payment terms match invoice
- [ ] IP ownership clause included
- [ ] Client confirmed acceptance in writing
- [ ] Stored in Google Drive

---

## 8. Approval Required

Founder must review before sending.

---

## 9. Output

- Signed or confirmed project agreement

---

## 10. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Clients / [ClientName] / Agreements /`

---

## 11. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **No scope boundary** — clients will keep adding features if you don't define what's included
- **No payment pause clause** — if client doesn't pay mid-project, you need documented right to pause
- **Overly complex legal contract** — clients resist signing elaborate contracts for small projects. Keep it simple.

---

## 12. Review Frequency

Quarterly. Have a lawyer review annually.
