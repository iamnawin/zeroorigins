# ZO-SALES-004 — Client Onboarding SOP

---

## 1. Purpose

To deliver a smooth, professional onboarding experience that sets clear expectations, collects all needed information, and makes the client feel confident they made the right choice.

---

## 2. Scope

All new clients from the moment they confirm a project (payment received) to the first deliverable.

---

## 3. Owner

Founder

---

## 4. Trigger

When advance payment is received from a new client.

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Send confirmation email (same day as payment)**
Subject: "You're confirmed — here's what happens next"

> "Hi [Name], great — payment received. You're officially on. Here's what the next few days look like:
>
> 1. I'll send you a short onboarding form today — please complete it within 24 hours.
> 2. We'll have a kickoff call on [proposed date] to align on everything.
> 3. Build starts on [date].
>
> Looking forward to working with you."

**Step 2: Send kickoff form (same day)**
The kickoff form collects everything needed to start work. Include:
- Access credentials needed (see ZO-AUTO-004 for API key handling)
- Current tool stack they use
- Specific pain points and priorities
- Key contact for this project
- Communication preference (WhatsApp, email, Slack)
- Who will test the workflow (technical or non-technical?)
- Any internal deadlines we should know about

**Step 3: Process payment and raise invoice**
- Raise invoice for advance payment (see ZO-FIN-001)
- Confirm invoice received

**Step 4: Log in Client Project Tracker**
Add new row to Client Project Tracker:
- Client name
- Project name
- Start date
- Expected delivery date
- Status: Onboarding

**Step 5: Review kickoff form responses**
- Read responses carefully
- Identify any risks or unclear requirements
- Note questions to clarify on kickoff call

**Step 6: Run kickoff call (within 3 days of payment)**
Kickoff call agenda (20–30 minutes):
1. Confirm project goal: "We're building [X] so that [outcome]."
2. Review timeline and milestones
3. Clarify any unclear requirements from the form
4. Agree on communication channel and cadence
5. Confirm who will approve the final delivery
6. Explain what the client needs to do (provide access, review, test)

**Step 7: Share project brief**
After kickoff call, send a 1-page project brief email confirming:
- What we're building (specific)
- Timeline and milestones
- Communication channel
- What client must provide and when
- What happens if requirements change mid-project

---

## 6. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Confirmation email sent within 1 hour of payment
- [ ] Onboarding form sent same day
- [ ] Invoice raised for advance
- [ ] Project logged in Client Project Tracker
- [ ] Kickoff call completed within 3 days
- [ ] Project brief sent after kickoff call
- [ ] All credentials received securely (see ZO-AUTO-004)

---

## 7. Approval Required

None.

---

## 8. Output

- Confirmed client record in Project Tracker
- Project brief agreed with client
- All credentials received
- Ready to begin build

---

## 9. Storage Location

Google Drive → `ZeroOrigins / Clients / [ClientName] / `

---

## 10. Risks / Mistakes to Avoid

- **Starting build before kickoff call** — scope changes after build starts cost double the time
- **Not documenting agreements** — verbal agreements evaporate; always send written project brief
- **Not collecting credentials early** — nothing stalls a project faster than waiting for an API key

---

## 11. Review Frequency

Quarterly.
