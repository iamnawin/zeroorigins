================================================================================
  START HERE — YOUR 5-STEP LAUNCH FLOW
  ZeroOrigins Pitch & Strategy Pack
================================================================================

You've got the pack. Now let's get it working for you.

Follow this flow in order. Each step builds on the last.
Don't skip ahead — the sequence matters.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  STEP 1 — UNDERSTAND YOUR OFFER CLEARLY
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Before you write a single proposal, you need clarity on what you're selling.

  Open: Templates/one_page_offer_template.md

  Fill it in. This forces you to define:
    → Who you serve
    → What outcome you deliver
    → How you deliver it
    → What it costs
    → Why you and not someone else

  Time required: 30–60 minutes
  Output: A clear one-page offer you can reference in every conversation

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  STEP 2 — RUN A DISCOVERY CALL (OR REVIEW A PAST CONVERSATION)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Open: Templates/client_discovery_questions.md

  Use these questions in your next client call, or work through them
  retrospectively if you already have a lead.

  The goal: understand the client's situation well enough to write a
  proposal that solves a real problem — not just lists your services.

  Time required: 45–90 minute call, or 20 minutes reviewing existing info
  Output: Clear notes you'll use to write your proposal

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  STEP 3 — WRITE YOUR PROPOSAL
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Open: Templates/proposal_template.md

  Before writing, also read:
    → Guide/example_completed_proposal.md  (see what a finished one looks like)
    → Resources/proposal_copy_examples.md  (strong vs weak writing examples)

  Customize every section for your specific client.
  Do not send a generic proposal. Personalization wins deals.

  Time required: 1–3 hours per proposal
  Output: A professional proposal ready to send

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  STEP 4 — ATTACH YOUR SCOPE DOCUMENT AND PRICING
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Open: Templates/scope_document_template.md
  Open: Templates/service_pricing_structure.md

  The scope document protects you legally and sets expectations.
  The pricing structure helps you think through your numbers properly.

  Send these alongside your proposal or as a second follow-up document.

  Time required: 30–60 minutes
  Output: Scope doc + clear pricing document

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  STEP 5 — CLOSE THE DEAL AND KICK OFF PROPERLY
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Once the client says yes:

  Open: Templates/project_kickoff_template.md
  Open: Guide/onboarding_checklist.md

  Use these to start every project professionally.
  This is what separates one-off gigs from long-term client relationships.

  Time required: 1 hour to prepare
  Output: Smooth project start, no confusion, happy client from day one
