# Tools List — Business & Client Communication Stack

*Curated tools for freelancers, consultants, founders, and agencies.*
*Organized by function. Includes free and paid options.*

---

## 1. PROPOSAL & DOCUMENT CREATION

### Better Proposals
**Use for:** Professional, trackable proposals with e-signature
**Why it's good:** Real-time notification when client opens your proposal. Signature built in.
**Pricing:** From $19/month
**betterproposals.io**

### Notion
**Use for:** Proposals, SOPs, client wikis, internal docs
**Why it's good:** Flexible, shareable, looks clean. Works well for async teams.
**Pricing:** Free plan available; Teams from $10/user/month
**notion.so**

### Google Docs
**Use for:** Quick proposals, editable collaboration, client review
**Why it's good:** Universal. Everyone can open it. Easy to track comments.
**Pricing:** Free
**docs.google.com**

### Qwilr
**Use for:** Beautiful web-based proposals with interactive pricing
**Why it's good:** Client-facing proposals that look like landing pages. Trackable.
**Pricing:** From $35/month
**qwilr.com**

### PandaDoc
**Use for:** Proposals, contracts, e-signatures, payment collection
**Why it's good:** All-in-one proposal + signature + payment. Good automation.
**Pricing:** From $19/month
**pandadoc.com**

## 2. E-SIGNATURES & CONTRACTS

### DocuSign
**Use for:** Legally binding e-signatures on contracts
**Why it's good:** Industry standard. Most enterprise clients will have it.
**Pricing:** From $10/month
**docusign.com**

### HelloSign (Dropbox Sign)
**Use for:** Simple e-signatures
**Why it's good:** Simpler and cheaper than DocuSign. Free for 3 docs/month.
**Pricing:** Free tier; paid from $15/month
**sign.dropbox.com**

### Zoho Sign
**Use for:** E-signatures, affordable for Indian market
**Why it's good:** Strong free tier, compliant with Indian IT Act.
**Pricing:** Free plan available
**zoho.com/sign**

---

## 3. SCHEDULING & BOOKING

### Calendly
**Use for:** Discovery calls, client meetings, consultation bookings
**Why it's good:** No-friction booking. Integrates with Google Calendar, Zoom, Teams.
**Pricing:** Free plan (limited); paid from $10/month
**calendly.com**

### Cal.com
**Use for:** Open-source scheduling alternative
**Why it's good:** Self-hostable, no branding on free plan, generous features.
**Pricing:** Free; Pro from $12/month
**cal.com**

### TidyCal
**Use for:** Simple, one-time fee booking tool
**Why it's good:** $29 lifetime deal (as of 2024). Good for solopreneurs.
**Pricing:** One-time fee
**tidycal.com**

## 4. PROJECT MANAGEMENT

### Notion
**Use for:** Client project tracking, SOPs, deliverable checklists
**Why it's good:** Flexible enough to be your CRM, PM tool, and doc hub.

### Trello
**Use for:** Simple Kanban-style project tracking
**Why it's good:** Visual, easy for clients to use too. Free plan is strong.
**Pricing:** Free; paid from $5/user/month
**trello.com**

### ClickUp
**Use for:** Full project management, time tracking, client portals
**Why it's good:** Very powerful. Free plan is generous.
**Pricing:** Free; paid from $7/user/month
**clickup.com**

### Linear
**Use for:** Engineering-team project management
**Why it's good:** Fast, opinionated. Great for dev-heavy work.
**Pricing:** Free for small teams
**linear.app**

## 5. INVOICING & PAYMENTS

### Stripe
**Use for:** Online payments, subscriptions, invoicing (international)
**Why it's good:** Globally trusted. Easy to embed. Good dashboard.
**Pricing:** 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
**stripe.com**

### Razorpay
**Use for:** Indian market payments, invoicing, subscriptions
**Why it's good:** Best option for INR transactions. Payment pages are quick to set up.
**Pricing:** 2% per transaction
**razorpay.com**

### Wave
**Use for:** Free invoicing and accounting (small businesses)
**Why it's good:** Completely free invoicing, decent reporting.
**Pricing:** Free
**waveapps.com**

### Zoho Invoice
**Use for:** Professional invoicing, GST-ready (India)
**Why it's good:** Free for up to 1 client. GST invoices built in.
**Pricing:** Free plan available
**zoho.com/invoice**

## 6. CLIENT COMMUNICATION

### Loom
**Use for:** Video walkthroughs, async client updates, proposal explanations
**Why it's good:** Record a 3-minute video instead of writing a long email. Huge time saver.
**Pricing:** Free plan (25 videos); paid from $12.50/month
**loom.com**

### Slack
**Use for:** Ongoing client communication (if they prefer it)
**Why it's good:** Organized by channels. Good for longer projects.
**Pricing:** Free; paid from $7.25/user/month
**slack.com**

### WhatsApp Business
**Use for:** Informal client communication (India, Middle East, Southeast Asia)
**Why it's good:** Where most clients already are. Quick, familiar.
**Pricing:** Free (API has costs for automation)
**business.whatsapp.com**

## 9. TIME TRACKING (FOR HOURLY BILLING)

### Toggl Track
**Use for:** Tracking hours per project and client
**Why it's good:** Simple, accurate, integrates with many tools. Free for individuals.
**Pricing:** Free; paid from $9/user/month
**toggl.com/track**

### Harvest
**Use for:** Time tracking + invoicing in one tool
**Why it's good:** Good for agencies and consultants who bill hourly.

**getharvest.com**

### n8n
**Use for:** Workflow automation, lead routing, integrations

**n8n.io**

### Zapier
**Use for:** Connecting apps without code
**Why it's good:** Largest integration library. Easy for non-technical users.

**zapier.com**

### Make (formerly Integromat)

**make.com**
