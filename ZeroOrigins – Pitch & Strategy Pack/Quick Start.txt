  QUICK START — ACTION IN THE NEXT 2 HOURS
  ZeroOrigins Pitch & Strategy Pack
================================================================================

Already have a client in mind? A proposal to write? A pitch to send this week?

Skip the full onboarding. Here's your fast-track path.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SCENARIO A: "I need to send a proposal TODAY."
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    1. Open Guide/example_completed_proposal.md
       Read the whole thing. (10 minutes)

    2. Open Templates/proposal_template.md
       Fill in every [BRACKET] with your real information. (60–90 minutes)

    3. Open Resources/proposal_copy_examples.md
       Check your language against the strong/weak examples. (15 minutes)

    4. Send it.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SCENARIO B: "I have a discovery call tomorrow."
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    1. Open Templates/client_discovery_questions.md
       Print or copy the questions you need. (10 minutes)

    2. Open Resources/client_red_flags.md
       Know what to watch for on the call. (10 minutes)

    3. Run the call. Take notes.

    4. Come back and write the proposal using Templates/proposal_template.md.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SCENARIO C: "I'm pitching a new service I've never sold before."
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    1. Open Templates/one_page_offer_template.md
       Define your offer in one page before anything else. (45 minutes)

    2. Open Resources/positioning_frameworks.md
       Choose a framework that matches your service type. (20 minutes)

    3. Open Templates/service_pricing_structure.md
       Decide your pricing model and set your numbers. (30 minutes)

    4. Now write your proposal or pitch deck.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SCENARIO D: "I just closed a deal. Now what?"
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    1. Open Templates/project_kickoff_template.md
       Customize and send to client within 24 hours of signing. (30 minutes)

    2. Open Guide/onboarding_checklist.md
       Work through the checklist before the first working session. (20 minutes)

    3. Open Templates/scope_document_template.md
       If you haven't sent a scope doc yet — send one now before work begins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
