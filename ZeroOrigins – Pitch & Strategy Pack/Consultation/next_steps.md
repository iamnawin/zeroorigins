## WHEN TO REACH OUT FOR SUPPORT

Consider getting 1:1 support if you:

- Have an active proposal situation and want a second pair of eyes before you send
- Are pricing a service type you've never sold before and want validation
- Have a difficult client situation in progress and need strategic advice
- Want help customizing the templates for a highly specific niche or service
- Want someone to review your positioning before you start outreach
- Are building an agency and need to create a repeatable proposal process for your team
