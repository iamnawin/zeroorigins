## 6. Revision Policy

| Round | Included | Scope |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Round 1 | ✓ Included | Minor changes, adjustments, corrections within the agreed direction |
| Round 2 | ✓ Included | Second round of consolidated feedback |
| Round 3+ | Billed separately | At [RATE] per hour or agreed flat fee per round |

**What counts as a revision:**
- Text changes, color adjustments, layout tweaks, copy edits

**What counts as a new scope request (not a revision):**
- Change of direction after approval
- New features or deliverables not in this scope
- Fundamental redesign of previously approved work

*If a revision request changes the project direction significantly, a change order will be issued before work proceeds.*

## 7. Change Order Process

If additional work is needed outside this scope:

1. Client submits written request describing additional work
2. Provider reviews and issues a Change Order within [2] business days

4. Work begins only after written approval of Change Order
