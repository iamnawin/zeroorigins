# Client Discovery Questions

**How to use this document:**

Run through these questions during a 45–90 minute discovery call.
You don't need to ask every question — pick what's relevant to the project.
Your goal is to understand their world well enough to write a proposal
that speaks to their real problem, not a generic one.

Take notes directly in this document during the call, then use those notes
to write the "Understanding Your Situation" section of your proposal.

> I'll send over a detailed proposal by [DATE]. Does that timeline work for you?"

- [ ] Their email address for proposal delivery

- [ ] Whether they have any other conversations happening (are they evaluating others?)
