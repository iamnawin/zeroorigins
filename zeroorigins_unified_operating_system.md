# ZeroOrigins — Unified Operating System
_Last updated: 2026-04-30_

## 1. What ZeroOrigins Is

**ZeroOrigins** is the parent AI-native company and umbrella brand.

It is the main company identity under which different business verticals operate.  
The goal is to keep all work aligned under one clear structure so that:
- each vertical has its own bucket
- services, products, media, and education do not get mixed
- automations and systems can be reused across the company
- strategy, GTM, content, and execution all stay connected

ZeroOrigins should be positioned as:
- a creative-tech company
- an AI systems and product company
- a studio for automation, apps, and digital solutions
- a long-term umbrella for multiple verticals

### Role of each vertical

#### Studio / Services
This is the service/business arm.
Use this for:
- AI automation services
- workflow design
- AI agent implementation
- client system setup
- consulting and strategy work
- design + build work for founders and businesses

#### SaaS / Products
This is for productized software.
Use this for:
- internal SaaS ideas
- paid subscription tools
- niche AI products
- B2B micro-SaaS
- product experiments that can become standalone businesses

#### Apps / Tools
This is for smaller apps, utilities, prototypes, and tools.
Use this for:
- demos
- internal utilities
- MVPs
- public showcaseable tools
- free tools and beta products

#### Automation Systems
This is the systems backbone.
Use this for:
- n8n workflows
- process automations
- lead capture systems
- internal ops systems
- alerts, reminders, routing, CRM sync
- workflow documentation and reusable templates

#### Client Solutions
This is where executed work gets organized by customer/project.
Use this for:
- discovery docs
- proposals
- scope docs
- delivery plans
- client assets
- deployment notes
- case studies

#### AIwithNoBrain
Separate creative/media vertical.
Use this for:
- AI music
- creative AI experiments
- viral content
- entertainment
- audio/visual identity work
- character/content IP

#### EpicsToYou
Separate storytelling/cinematic vertical.
Use this for:
- AI-generated videos
- mythological and cinematic storytelling
- YouTube / reels content systems
- visual storytelling IP
- scripts, prompts, music/video pipelines

#### IgnAIte
Do **not** use this as the main company right now.
Reserve it for:
- academy
- workshops
- bootcamps
- AI training
- career-oriented learning products
- beginner-to-builder education later

## 3. Core Strategic Decision

### Main operating brand right now
Use **ZeroOrigins** as the main company for:
- SaaS
- AI tools
- automation systems
- client services
- design/build work
- product experiments
- systems and operations

### Do not force everything under IgnAIte
Reason:
- IgnAIte naturally sounds like an institute/academy/training brand
- it works well for education, workshops, and bootcamps
- it is not the best primary container for all SaaS, automation, and product work

### Practical conclusion
- **ZeroOrigins** = main company
- **IgnAIte** = later education/training vertical
- **AIwithNoBrain** = creative/music vertical
- **EpicsToYou** = AI video/cinematic vertical

## 5. Company Operating Logic

### Rule 1
Each idea must go into the correct bucket.

### Rule 2
Do not mix education, SaaS, services, and media randomly.

### Rule 3
All automations and systems should be reusable across the company wherever possible.

### Rule 4
ZeroOrigins is the master strategic layer.

### Rule 5
Verticals can have their own content, offers, and GTM, but the backend systems should be unified where useful.
