==============================================================
  WORKFLOW NOTES — READ BEFORE IMPORTING
==============================================================

These notes tell you exactly what to change in each workflow
after you import it into n8n. Nothing is hardcoded — every
personal value is clearly marked with [YOUR_VALUE_HERE].

--------------------------------------------------------------
LEAD CAPTURE WORKFLOW (lead_capture.json)
--------------------------------------------------------------

After import, open the workflow and update the following nodes:

  NODE: Google Sheets — "Log Lead to Sheet"
    - Connect your Google account credential
    - Spreadsheet ID: paste your Google Sheet ID
      (found in the URL: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[THIS_PART]/edit)
    - Sheet name: change "Leads" to whatever you named your sheet tab

  NODE: Gmail — "Notify Owner"
    - Connect your Gmail credential
    - To: change to YOUR email address (where you want lead alerts)
    - From Name: change "ZeroOrigins" to your business name

  NODE: Gmail — "Send Confirmation to Lead"
    - From Name: change "ZeroOrigins" to your business name
    - Subject: customise if needed
    - Body: update the booking link and your name/brand

  NODE: Respond to Webhook
    - No changes needed unless you want a custom success message

WEBHOOK URL:
  After activating, copy the webhook URL from the Webhook node.
  This URL goes into your form tool as the "submission URL" or
  "webhook destination". Every form submission triggers this workflow.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FOLLOW-UP WORKFLOW (followup_flow.json)
--------------------------------------------------------------

  NODE: Schedule Trigger
    - Default: runs daily at 9:00 AM
    - Change the time to suit your timezone

  NODE: Google Sheets — "Read Pending Leads"
    - Same credential and Sheet ID as above
    - The workflow filters for rows where "Followup Sent" = FALSE
      and the lead is at least 24 hours old

  NODE: Gmail — "Send Follow-Up Email"
    - Connect the same Gmail credential
    - From Name: update to your business name
    - Body: personalise the follow-up message and your CTA/booking link

  NODE: Google Sheets — "Mark as Followed Up"
    - Same credential and Sheet ID
    - This node writes TRUE to the "Followup Sent" column
      and today's date to "Followup Date" — do not change the logic

--------------------------------------------------------------
GOOGLE SHEET COLUMN ORDER (REQUIRED — DO NOT CHANGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------

  Column A: Timestamp
  Column B: Name
  Column C: Email
  Column D: Business
  Column E: Message
  Column F: Source
  Column G: Status          (default value: "New")
  Column H: Followup Sent   (default value: FALSE)
  Column I: Followup Date   (default value: blank)

  The workflows use column letters to read/write data.
  If you add columns, add them AFTER column I.
