==============================================================
  QUICK START — ZEROORIGINS WORKFLOW STARTER KIT
  Read this if you want to be live in under 60 minutes.
==============================================================

You need 3 accounts before you begin:
  - n8n (free at n8n.io — cloud or self-hosted)
  - Google account (for Sheets + Gmail)
  - A form tool (Tally, Typeform, or any form with webhook support)

--------------------------------------------------------------
THE 5-STEP SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------

  1. CREATE YOUR GOOGLE SHEET
     Make a new Google Sheet with these columns in row 1:
       Timestamp | Name | Email | Business | Message | Source | Status | Followup Sent | Followup Date

  2. IMPORT BOTH WORKFLOWS INTO N8N
     In n8n: click "+" → Import from file
     Import lead_capture.json first, then followup_flow.json

  3. CONNECT YOUR ACCOUNTS IN N8N
     In each workflow, click the Google Sheets node and Gmail node.
     Connect your Google account when prompted.
     Paste your Sheet ID into the Google Sheets node.
     (Your Sheet ID is the long string in the Sheet URL)

  4. ACTIVATE THE WEBHOOK
     Open lead_capture workflow → click "Activate"
     Copy the webhook URL shown at the top of the Webhook node.
     Paste it as the submission URL in your form tool.

  5. SEND A TEST LEAD
     Fill out your form once with your own details.
     Check your Google Sheet — a new row should appear.
     Check your email — you should receive a confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------
THAT'S IT. YOUR SYSTEM IS LIVE.
--------------------------------------------------------------

If any step above doesn't work:
